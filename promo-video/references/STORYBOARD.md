# From a website to a storyboard

## 1. Facts sheet (before any idea)
Read the source the person gives (site, blog, marketplace profile, About page). Collect, with the sentence it came from:
- what they do in one line (the site's own headline is usually the best hook),
- the process (numbered steps), promises (no hidden fees, no sign-up, weekends too, recommends the cheaper domestic option…),
- proof that is **on the page** (registrations, credentials, years, former roles), and contact (site, phone, chat channel),
- the customer's pain in their words (blog comments, FAQ, Q&A posts).
Anything not on the page stays out. Example prices or results are labelled "예시 / example". Blogs that block fetching
usually have an RSS feed.

## 2. The arc (15–40 s, 5–7 scenes)
| beat | share | job | example (minimal, 30 s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| hook | 0–4 s | the viewer's own situation, in their words | SNS post: "이거 어디서 팔아요?" |
| problem | ~4 s | what makes it hard / costly | search finds nothing; foreign sites, payment, shipping |
| solution | ~4 s | the service as the obvious answer, headline from the site | send one photo → "찾으시는 상품, 해외에서 찾아드려요" |
| how / proof | 8–10 s | the process made visible, promises as stamps/chips | quote card with "숨은 비용 없음", pay → inspect → ship |
| trust | ~4 s | the one honest, surprising promise | "국내가 더 저렴하면, 국내 구매를 권해드려요" |
| CTA | ~4 s | logo, one action, contact | button tap "상품 찾기 요청 →", site · chat · phone |
Shorts (pop): same arc in 18–20 s, one idea per beat, the hook in the first second.

## 3. Proposal to send (before building)
1. **Recommendation** — one style and why it fits this audience/channel (see STYLES.md), in 2–3 sentences.
2. **Storyboard** — a table per orientation or one table with a portrait note: scene · time · what is on screen ·
   on-screen text · narration line · sound. A contact-sheet image of rough frames helps a lot.
3. **Alternatives** — 3–4 other styles with "suits whom / mood / channel".
4. What you need from them (logo file, photo they own, phone number to show), and what you will not show (invented stats).
Build only after a yes; when the person says "all", build all, one page each, sharing `assets/`.

## 4. Copy rules
- One message per scene; headline ≤ 2 lines; the brand name appears early as a small bug and big only at the end.
- Write to the viewer ("사진 한 장만 보내세요"), not about the company. Korean ads: 해요체 narration, short lines.
- The narration says it, the screen shows it — not the same words twice; screen text can be shorter.
