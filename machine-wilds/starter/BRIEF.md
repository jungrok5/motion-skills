# Brief — Lantern Quarry

## The place
A granite quarry, flooded since the pumps stopped; two machines that lit the night shift still keep their lanterns on.
It was a working quarry cut in three benches; when it was abandoned the wardens were left running; thirty years on,
pine and birch stand to the rim.

## The ways and the set piece
The rim way circles the pit 44–50 m out. From the landing the walker looks across the water at the standing warden
when it lowers its lantern, while the other passes behind on the rim.
