# Brief — 쇠울림 골짜기 (Soeullim, "the valley where iron rings")

## The place
A wooded valley forty years after people left it; the machines they built to watch it, carry for it and relay its
signals have never been told to stop.
It was a maintenance district on a mountain road: a viaduct over the valley, a hall where the machines were serviced, a
yard of containers, a power line along the north ridge. The road was cut when the watchers, re-routed by no one,
walked through one span of the viaduct. Spruce and pine closed over everything else.

## The ways and the set piece
- **round** (cranes, 900 m, 15 m wide): down the valley floor south of the brook, through the viaduct's gap, round the
  tarn's bog, then along the high shelf south of the hall, across the old road, past the way in (17 m from where the
  walker starts) and down the western slope. It crosses the viaduct's line once through the gap and once beyond its
  south abutment — never under a standing span (the crane is taller than the deck). The walker crosses it on the path
  down to the fallen crane and on the road, and stands above it on the viaduct.
- **bank** (porters, 460 m, 13 m wide): a loop on the north shelf past the yard, crossing the old road north of the
  viaduct's abutment.
- **rim** (the relic, 1500 m, 38 m wide): round the whole valley at 240 m; from the walk it is lit windows and two
  eyes in the mist, and, where it passes the way in, legs between the trees.
- **brook** (the crayfish, 300 m, 13 m wide): the valley floor west of the viaduct, down one side of the brook and back
  up the other, through the water twice; its east end comes within forty metres of the fallen crane, so the walker
  who goes down the path to the wreck meets two red eyes in the mist beyond it.
- **east** (a turtle, 114 m, 14 m wide): a small round on the level floor far to the east, across the brook and back;
  it draws its head in beside the water. A second turtle is a post on the tarn's western shore (97, 82).
- **shelf** (woodlice, 178 m, 3.4 m wide): the lower shelf south of the floor, between the cranes' way and the bank
  under the old road — a file of four that the walker looks down on from the road and can walk among.
- **sky** (kites, 910 m, 67 s a lap): a wide ring 55–80 m up that swings in low over the viaduct and the fallen crane
  and climbs away over the banks — no turn tighter than forty metres, never under 43 m over the land. From the walk
  they are five beams combing the mist, coming round about once a minute, and a cry.
- **The set piece**: the southern half of the viaduct, walked out to the broken edge, barriers across it. A crane
  comes through the gap 26 m away with its back level with the walker's feet, stops there, and lifts its head to look.
  The walker may go to it: a hop clears the barriers (and the parapets along the sides), the broken edge is a drop of
  fifteen metres to the valley floor, taken as a fall and a landing; the way back up is the path by the hall.

## The hour, the walk, the words
Opens at 20:24 in mist (the mist holds a little light of its own after dark: shapes, not murk). The round: mist at dusk → night → a grey dawn → clear noon → rain → mist again. Spawn at the
way in looking down the road. Tour (641 m): the road, the viaduct to its edge and back, the hall, the path down to the
valley floor and the fallen crane, up again. Nine views. Sounds: the brook, frogs at the tarn, the fire, footfalls and
calls. Korean.
