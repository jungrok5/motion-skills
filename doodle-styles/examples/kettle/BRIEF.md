# 저녁 주전자 — brief

Topic given: "a kettle coming to the boil for a small tea shop's winter card — something that feels warm".
Neither the anidoodle style plates nor this skill's other example draw it; this piece proves the topic → brief → scene →
any hand path.

## The point
- **Idea in one sentence**: on a cold evening the kettle is about to boil, and the tea is already poured — warmth is coming.
- **Focal subject**: the red enamel kettle, steam leaving its spout.
- **The light**: the evening window on the RIGHT (warm sky, the only source); the kettle and mug turn toward it; shadows
  fall left on the steel stove top. The window is a `glow`; its light lands on the stove top in front of it as a `highlight` (not on its own wall — a window cannot light the wall it is in).

## The hand
- **Style**: `woodblock` *(mine)* — a winter card wants a keepsake print; flat blocks carry a red kettle against a sage
  wall, and the keyblock-then-colour film is a good making-of. Runners-up shown on the sheet: `watercolour` (warmer,
  softer), `risograph` (louder, for social).
- **Settings**: defaults.

## Format
- **Size**: 1080×1350 (4:5) — an Instagram card and a printed postcard *(mine)*.
- **Film**: drawing 7 s, hold 1 s, life 4 s, looping.
- **Life**: the steam keeps rising and swaying (three ribbons, phase-shifted, periodic over 4 s). Nothing else moves:
  the stillness of the room is the point.

## The subject, built from its real structure
- background: sage plaster wall `#7d958c` (`bg`); a cream window frame with a mullion and transom (white lines drawn as
  light); the evening sky `#f2c879` (`glow`) with far roofs `#8a6f73` against it.
- ground: dark steel stove top `#4f555c` (`ground`), its front edge a dark line; a black burner grate (ellipse seen from
  above).
- kettle: a lathe profile — wide flat belly, sloping shoulder, a lid ring and a black knob — `#c8402e`, `form` 0.95; a
  spout as a tapering tube rising to the left; a dark bail handle over the top; a cream enamel band on the belly.
- its cast shadow: to the left, on the grate (`shadow`, strength 0.6).
- the mug: a cylinder with an elliptical rim (`#efe6d2`), tea surface `#5b3a28`, a C-handle on the right, its shadow.
- light lines: three steam ribbons `#f4efe6` (light, so every hand draws them as light), the white window bars.

## Reference
- An enamel stovetop kettle (wide-base whistling kettle, bail handle) and a ceramic mug, from product photos; the
  window light falling on the wall below a window at dusk.
