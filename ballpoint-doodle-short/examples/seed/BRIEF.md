# BRIEF — 「Water the Seed」

**Topic (as given):** "a little mole who is asked to look after a seed"

**Logline:** Gran plants a seed and a sign — *water the seed*. Momo waters it, hums, looks away; a gust takes the seed.
He chases it down the hill, across the pond, takes it back from a crow with a splash of his watering can — and loses
it over a cliff. At the bottom, a tear and the last drop from the can fall on the soil, and the seed comes up a
sunflower. Back home, there are now five seeds, and the sign says *seeds*.

distance / radius), skips on lily pads, rides in a beak, falls, and grows (`sunflower()`).
