# The look — what makes it read as this style

Observed from the source clip (Kevin Ngo on X, https://x.com/kevin_t_ngo/status/2121261917016903748 — "Every frame
drawn with JavaScript", 80 s, 1080×1080, a bird told to "watch the egg" chasing it across a doodled world). These
are the style's rules; its characters, egg, snake and gags are **not** to be reused — design new ones per topic.

## The sheet
- One cream sheet (≈ rgb 242, 234, 221), faint mottling and tooth, no frame, no border, no sky colour. Most of every
  frame is bare paper; the drawing lives in the lower half on one line.
- The sun (yellow crayon in an orange ballpoint ring, short rays) is pinned to the page, not the world.

## Two hands
- **Ballpoint** for every outline: thin (≈ 2.5 px at 1080), navy-blue for creatures and signs, red for the ground, green
  for plants, brown for wood and soil, blue for water. A slight wobble, a second lighter pass that never quite lands on
  the first, open ends that overshoot a little. Lines breathe (re-drawn 8× a second) but stay where they are.
- **Coloured pencil** for every fill: parallel strokes leaning like a right hand's (≈ −55°), uneven pressure, paper
  showing between the strokes, a lighter even layer beneath, a denser crescent of the same pencil on the side away from
  the light (lower right). The fill stops a hair short of the line and sits slightly off it.
- Solid black only for eyes (a glossy bean with two white highlights) and music notes.

## The line
- The ground is a single red ballpoint line that the camera follows. Its *shape* is the place: flat at home, tilted for a
  hill, blue (with little ~ marks under it) for water, vertical for a cliff (hatched in red pencil on its face).
- Things stand on it: grass tufts (three green strokes), ferns, a mushroom, rocks in grey pencil, a stump, a flower.
  Under every standing creature a small grey ballpoint scribble shadow.

## The creatures
- Round eggs on two small feet; arms are short wings or paws; one or two tells (a hat, a scarf, hairs on top, a tail).
- Big glossy black eyes, pink blush ovals in pencil, a beak / button nose; expressions carried by lids (the half-lidded
  unimpressed look is a staple), brows and a small mouth.
- They turn as balls: profile shows one eye at the front edge and the tail at the back; the back view has no face.

## Motion
- 24 fps; drawings on twos (poses, faces, effects change 12× a second); positions along the line and the camera smooth.
- Squash on landings, stretch on jumps; anticipation before a run; hops arc.
- Pen effects act: surprise rays and "!", speed lines, sweat drops, music notes, dizzy stars, puffs, splashes, gust curls.

## Story and sound
- Wordless except one handwritten sign that sets the errand; it returns, changed, at the end (a loop).
- A chase through 3–5 differently shaped places, one gag each; a threat; the loss (music drops out); a turn; home again.
- Score: a bouncy small band (pizzicato, marimba, whistle) with foley on every beat (boing, pop, splash, whoosh, crack),
  quiet for the sad beat, the tune again for the loop.
