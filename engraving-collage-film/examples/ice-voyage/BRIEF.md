# 얼음 항해 (The Ice Voyage) — BRIEF

ONE LINE: The cook's daughter climbs down from a ship frozen into the pack ice, runs and jumps an open lead, and that
night waves at the aurora.

## World
- where / when: the polar sea of the old expeditions, a pale day and then a night — engraved ice, ships and skies are
  the great Victorian expedition-book pictures.
- sets:
  - `floe`: pack ice (kit `ground` 'ice'), a three-master frozen in with its sails furled (kit `ship`, sunk into the ice,
    rolled 3°), icebergs standing off (new subject `iceberg`: a jagged block split into lit facets in sparse vertical
    lines and shaded ones crossing, snow on the crests, a dark waterline), small floes near the lens, a far range (new
    `mountains`: snow slopes white, shade sides hatched at 60°), the sky ring, two drifting clouds.
  - `lead`: the same floe with an open lead across it (new `lead`: black water laid flat, ragged edges, floating ice
    chips) and a berg that rises as she passes.
  - `night`: the same floe darker under the aurora (new `auroraSky`: a dark line-screen sky, three white curtains with
    vertical streaks, star points), haze low.
