## What it shows, beat by beat
| time | stage chip | what happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | ラフ (rough) | Thin blue lines on white: a head circle and a centre line. A white stylus draws each line; its shadow falls on the paper. |
| 2–22 s | 線画 (line art) | The blue construction keeps growing for a few seconds (cross for the eye line, ear ovals, jaw, neck, shoulders, a curtain edge), then dark tapered strokes arrive one at a time: the eyes with heavy upper lids, the brows, the nose and mouth, the jaw, then dozens of hair strands from the crown outward, the collar. The blue stays visible underneath, fading. |
| 22–24 s | 下塗り (flats) | Base colours brushed in with a ragged edge: brown hair, skin, shirt. |
| 24–30 s | 影 (shadow) | A dark wall behind; darker shapes painted over the flats: under the bangs, the side of the face, the neck, inside the hair. |
| 30–36 s | ハイライト (highlight) | The hair lightens to blond in sweeps; light on the cheek, nose and neck; the curtain catches light. |
| 36–47 s | 仕上げ (finish) | Light and colour settle: soft light from the window side, the lines warm into the colours, the curtain's folds and glow, a final hold on the finished painting. |

The page: a beige background, 「メイキング再生」 (making replay) in bold at the top left, 「線も色もコードで描いています」
(both lines and colours are drawn in code) in small grey at the top right, six pill chips (the active one orange),
and a white card with rounded corners and a soft shadow.

## What this skill takes, and what it does not
| taken (the format) | not taken |
|---|---|
| the page layout: title, note, six chips, the card | the portrait: the blond young man, his face, hair and shirt |
| the six stages in order, and how each looks (blue rough, tapered ink, ragged brushed flats, painted shadows, highlights, a lit finish) | the picture's composition and colours |
| the stylus that follows the drawing tip | the Japanese UI text (this skill uses Korean or English labels; a translation of the same idea) |
| the rough fading under the line art; the long line stage and the short finish | the post's text, and the claim about tracing |

The engine, the drawings (the mug, the rainy-window girl) and the designs are this repo's own. Everything is drawn
from a brief, except in reference mode. That mode answers the post's question: it does draw toward a target image,
but only one the user owns or may use. It says so in the brief, and it still makes every mark as a code item
(references/REFERENCE.md).
