# 연못 — brief

**Topic:** a small pond — the starter: the fewest words that still make a complete world.
**The world in one sentence:** a pond seen from above: stones, lotus pads that drift, carp that come to the brush.
**Title on the seal:** 연못 · **subtitle:** 글자 하나가 못 하나 · **language / script:** Korean, Hangul · **brush font:** YeonSung

## The sheet
- View: from above. The whole sheet is water; there is no horizon.
- Clockwork: none.
- Palette: paper `[.925, .905, .84]`, night `[.03, .045, .055]`; pigments 1 먹 (stones, carp), 2 주 (the carp's patches),
  3 청 (pads, moss).

## Sound
D major pentatonic by day, a darker five-note scale at night, 60 bpm. No voices of its own.

## Performance (51 s)
돌 → 연잎 → 잉어 → a stir without ink (the pads drift, the carp follow) → 비 → a black stroke → 밤 (fireflies) → a red
stroke → 낮 → 비움.
