# Brief: the light for each shot

Write one block per shot in the piece's `light/BRIEF.md` before any plan.

```
## <shot> (<file or page>, <start>–<end> s)
Picture:     what is in frame, where the eye should go
Light from:  sun high / low · in frame (where) / off frame (which edge) · time of day
Through:     what it passes — canopy gaps, a window, slats, a doorway, cloud
Job:         what the light does for the shot (lead the eye to …, show depth, a cold lonely morning …)
Beams:       how many main / thin, where they land
Pools:       on what surface, where
Colour:      the light's colour; the grade (warm / neutral / cool), haze
Strength:    a hint · clearly there · the shot is about it
Camera:      still · moves (how) → keyframes or a projected sun
```

## Review questions for the lit stills

1. Do all the beams come from one place, and is it where the shadows say the sun is?
2. Does each beam start at a gap the picture has, narrow there and widen where it lands?
3. Are widths and spacing uneven enough to read as light, not as stripes?
4. Do the pools sit where the beams land, flat on their surface?
5. Is the colour that of the hour in the brief, and the grade with it?
6. Are the brightest whites and the darkest shadows where they were (nothing washed, nothing blown)?
7. Does the light do the job the brief gave it — does the eye go where it should?
8. In motion: does the light stay on the sun while the camera moves; is the shimmer a breath, not a flicker?

A still that fails one of these is fixed in the plan before the shot is rendered.
