# BRIEF — 「Marble Canon」 (the template's brief; replace every line for your topic — references/BRIEF.md)

## 1. One line
A marimba spiral turns a D-major progression into running eighths while a glockenspiel tune, a bass marimba and four
singing bowls play on rings round it.

## 2. Music
- Source: written for the template (original). 12 bars, 4/4, ♩ = 96, D major — 30 s of bars (+3.2 s intro; the film is 35.7 s).
- Form: bars 1–4 the progression D – Bm – G – A as arpeggios, bass and the first bowl; bars 5–8 the glockenspiel tune
  enters; bars 9–11 Em – G – A, the tune climbs to A6; bar 12 D, everything lands, the last bowl.
- Events: bar 1 (first drops, 3.2 s), bar 5 (tune, 13.2 s), bar 9 (lift, 23.2 s), bar 12 (cadence, 30.7 s).
- Writing for balls: the arpeggios are unbroken eighths (one ball chains ~20 notes from 1.32 m); the tune's half notes
  start new balls; the bass walks in quarters on hard bars (e = 0.92, top 1.7 m) so one ball plays ~10.

## 4. Machine plan
Concentric: the arpeggio spiral at the centre (r 0.34 → ~1.2 m, top 1.32 m), the bowls' short arc at r 1.55, the tune's
ring at r 2.0, the bass ring at r 2.6 (top 1.7 m). From above: a canon of rings round a whirl. Footprint ~5.3 × 4.7 m.

## 4b. Chain of causes
Glockenspiel runs alternate droppers and catapults, the bass is all catapults (flung 1.7 m up to its ring), arp and bowls
drop. The starter hammer stands among the first three starts; from then on the engine finds every cause — in the
solved machine: 3 starter lines, 10 relays (a bowl's cup sets off the second arp ball; an arp cup sets off the tune), 9
strikes (bass pads push rods that release bowls and arp balls), 1 branch; 23 lines, ~1,100 dominoes. Shown: the starter
(0–2.2 s), the relay that wakes the tune (9.7 s), the bass relay to a catapult (21 s).
