# BRIEF — 「첫차」 (The First Bus)

## The scene (one line)
Five past five on a January morning, snow falling on a hillside bus stop: the first bus of the day comes up the road,
brakes, settles on its springs and opens its door, and the four people who have been waiting in the cold start to move.

## Why this topic, this image
- The moment: 05:05, mid-January, a residential hill in the outer city. It has snowed since midnight; the road has two
  dark tyre tracks, the pavement has a few footprints. The sky is still night but the cloud is lit orange from below by
  the city.
- The beat: headlights far away → they grow and light the falling snow → the bus stops with a nose-dip and rocks back →
  the door folds open and warm light spills onto the snow → the first person steps forward.
- What someone who has waited for a first bus checks first: breath you can see; the shelter's lit ad panel being the
  warmest thing around; people standing apart, each in their own coat; the long padded coat; snow only in the light;
  the bus being much bigger than you expect when it pulls in.
