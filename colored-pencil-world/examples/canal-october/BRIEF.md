# BRIEF — Canal in October

Topic (the test topic for this skill, not in the source): "a canal town in autumn — someone crossing a bridge with flowers".

## The picture in one line
On an October afternoon a young man in a mustard coat and a long red scarf walks the west quay of a canal under turning
limes, crosses the stone bridge with a bunch of flowers, stops on its crest to look down the water to the church tower,
and comes down onto the far quay — leaves coming down all the while.
