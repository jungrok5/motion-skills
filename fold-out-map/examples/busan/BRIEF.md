# Brief — 부산 Busan fold-out map

## The trip in one line
A day in Busan as a fold-out travel map: from the pastel hillside village to the lighthouse cliffs, the beach towers,
the temple on the sea rocks at golden hour, and Gwangan Bridge lit at night.

## The map
- Sheet: 4 × [0.85, 2] → 3.4 × 4 units (portrait).
- Projection: centre (129.112, 35.122), 6.6 km per unit → lon 128.99–129.24, lat 35.00–35.24.
- Coast: traced loosely from Dadaepo / Molundae east along the harbour, Gamman, Igidae, Gwangalli, Suyeong river mouth,
  Dongbaek, Haeundae, Cheongsapo, Songjeong, Yonggungsa, Gijang. Island: Yeongdo.
- Rivers: Suyeong-gang (to the bay), Oncheon-cheon.
- Hills: Geumjeongsan (north), Baegyangsan, Hwangnyeongsan, Gudeoksan, Cheonmasan (above Gamcheon), Bongnaesan (Yeongdo),
  Jangsan (above Haeundae), Igidae, the Gijang hills.
- Districts (grid angle): Jung-gu 24°, Seomyeon −8°, Dongnae 12°, Suyeong 32°, Haeundae −4°, Sasang 4°, Saha 40°,
  Yeongdo 30°, Gijang 20°, Centum −20°, Nam-gu 15°. Streets kept off water, rivers and steep slopes.
- Roads: Jungang-daero (Nampo → Dongnae), Suyeong-ro → Haeundae → Gijang, two western links; Gwangan Bridge and the
  harbour bridge drawn as bridges; the Gyeongbu line as rail.
- Names: 중구 서면 동래 수영 해운대 사상 사하 영도 기장 남구 with romanised names; hills with ▲; 수영강; 광안대교; 대한해협 / Korea Strait.
- Title: BUSAN (deep green, top left over the hills) + 부산 여행 지도 · A FOLD-OUT TRAVEL MAP. Legend (one-day route) in
  the open sea bottom left; compass and 5 km scale bar bottom right.
