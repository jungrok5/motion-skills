Broadside · No. 7

The river keeps its own hours

It does not hurry for the bridge,
or slow because the lamps are lit.

It takes the light the way it takes a leaf:
turns it over, carries it, lets it go.

Stand here long enough and learn
the only clock it keeps —

everything comes back,
never quite to the same place.
