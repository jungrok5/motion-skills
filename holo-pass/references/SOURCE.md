style ticket with an illustration in a stamp frame, a vertical title and a stub with a QR code; seasonal templates

add-to-calendar on the guest's screen; four steps — choose, fill in, decorate, send.

## What this skill takes, and what it does not

| taken (the format) | not taken |
|---|---|
| a ticket-shaped invitation with a holographic foil that follows the tilt | the product's name, domain, brand or page text |
| an illustration in a postage-stamp window that moves | the mascot character and every illustration |
| a vertical title + a stub with date / time / place / price / QR / to / from | the exact layouts, colours and copy of their templates |
| an envelope reveal → the pass flies out → flip to a postcard back | their music (this skill synthesises its own four loops) |
| music, RSVP, add-to-calendar on the guest's page | their code (this is written from scratch) |
| link / QR image / MP4 as ways to send | |

The six templates, the faces, the envelope, the timeline, the backgrounds and the tracks were designed in this repo.
