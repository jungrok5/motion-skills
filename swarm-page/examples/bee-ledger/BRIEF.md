# BRIEF — a beekeeper's honey ledger whose figures go out foraging as bees

## The page
- **What it is:** the weekly ledger of a small apiary (Linden Hill, seven hives), read by the beekeeper and the people who
  buy the honey. It is a working page: the headline and the notes are editable, the table sorts by any column.
- **Kicker / headline / note:** "Linden Hill apiary · Ledger, week 34" / "Seven hives, one long afternoon." / a short
  note on the lime flow ending and the heather to come.
- **Body:** a table of hives — Hive, Frames, Honey (kg), Temper — sortable (each header is a button; rows fly to their
  new places). A second short note with the next inspection date.
- **Controls:** fly "Open the hives" · land "Call them home" (held) · sound toggle (held).
- **Ink:** dark comb brown #2b1d0e on a pale gold sky. No second light.
- **Type:** Rockwell / Georgia-style slab for the headline (system stack), Georgia for text.

## The creature
- **Creature:** bee. Small, quick, busy: a striped body and a wing blur that flickers.
- **Mass and size:** light — speed 190, minSpeed 80, maxTurn 3.4 rad/s, follow springs 8–26 (quick to answer).
- **In the air:** tint #e2a51f × 0.78 (the brown ink turns amber-gold), blend over, haze #f2deb0.
- **Wingbeat:** 26 beats/s (reads as a flicker), glide 0.12 (bees barely glide; they hover).
- **Idle at rest:** hops every 1.2–3 s (a bee shifting on the comb), sheen 0.03.

## The flock
- **Formation:** ball — a loose swarm cloud, thick in every direction, spread 0.38.
- **Roams:** area 0.18 0.12 0.86 0.58, depth 0.3; wander high (1.1), cohesion high (2), alignment lower (3), salience 4.
- **Gather → lift:** gather 0.3 s, lift wave 1100 px/s from the button, kick 260.
- **Land:** order origin (they come back to the hive end of the page first) · span 1.6 s.

## Sky and setting
- **Hour:** late August afternoon, 5 pm: pale blue top, cream, gold low; sun at x 0.8 y 0.6 with a wide glow; light rays;
  a few soft clouds.
- **Scene in front:** a far line of limes and hedges, three hive boxes on stands at the left, lavender and grass along the
  bottom. No water.

## Trigger
- Fly: the "Open the hives" button. Land: "Call them home" or Escape. In flight the pointer is a hand the bees avoid;
  clicking where a line was calls those bees back so it can be read and typed into.

## Sound
- buzz (three detuned saws through a low-pass), ambience: birds.

## Still review
- rest: the ledger reads at a glance; hives and lavender sit below the table, nothing overlaps.
- fly 0.3 s: the figures near the button already lifting, the rest quivering.
- fly 3 s: one golden swarm in the sky with depth (near bees larger), the page empty except the held controls.
- land 1.2 s: bees streaming back to the hive end first; partly formed rows.
- land 5 s: the ledger whole again.
