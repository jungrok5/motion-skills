# North Pier

ONE LINE: a striped lighthouse throwing two beams over scalloped waves — for a harbour ferry line.

## Subject
- What it depicts: a lighthouse, front view, on the sea
- Why this subject: a ferry line's promise is "you'll find your way home"; the beams make it a light, not a tower
- Reading at 24 px: the tapered tower with its stripes and the two beams (a T-shaped silhouette)
- Facing / stance: front, symmetric; the tower stands on `base`
