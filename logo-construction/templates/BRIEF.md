# Ember Fox

ONE LINE: a fox in mid-stride, leaning forward — for a small-batch coffee roaster that roasts at dawn.

## Subject
- What it depicts: a red fox standing, facing right
- Why this subject: early, alert, a little wild; "ember" in the name, the fox's colour on the mockups
- Reading at 24 px: two pointed ears and the long muzzle; the tail block behind
- Facing / stance: right; four legs on the ground, the whole body leaning into the stride

## Grid
- Lean: 14° — the forward lean of a trot, shared by every slant (legs, rump, nape, chest, tail tip)
- Main slants: `rump` (back of body, back of hind leg), `nape` (back of neck *and* back of the first ear), `chest` (front of body and of the front leg)
- Horizontals: crown 300 · jaw 410 · back 470 · belly 600 · ground 790
- Free lines: `bridge` + `chin` (muzzle); `earF` and its parallel `ear2F`; `tailTop` parallel to `chin`; `tailBot`
- Circles: `eye` (cut) — moved down until it had ink all round (it was a notch at first)
- Families: `leg1…6` copied across; tail tip split by `tipCut` / `tipGap` slants (the gap reads as the white tip)
