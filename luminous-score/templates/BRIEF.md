## 1 · The piece
- **Title / composer**: "First Snow" — *for solo violin, strings and harpsichord*; engraved "luminous-score · template".
- **What part**: all of it — 14 bars of 4/4 at ♩ = 112, the last bar held at half speed → 32.1 s of music, 38.8 s with the title and the fade.
- **Notes from**: composed for this template (original). A small Baroque allegro in D minor: the strings enter one by one
  on pulsing eighths, the solo violin answers in sixteenth-note arpeggios with trills, everyone joins a unison run, a
  held last chord.
- **Rights**: original, no recording (synthesised).
