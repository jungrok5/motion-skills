# The look — what the source does, and what this skill keeps

Source: the post by 🎼🌺Music Love♥️ (@ThoNg676733) on X — https://x.com/ThoNg676733/status/2119610929654469035 —
"Vivaldi's 'Winter,' visualized … one can literally 'see' the music": a 3 min 38 s, 1280 × 720 film of the first movement
of Winter. Read from stills taken every 6 s.

## Observed

- **A real score, lying flat.** A full orchestral system printed on grey-lavender paper, seen in perspective at a low,
  raking angle. Staff lines, beams, stems, rests, dynamics (p, cresc.) and bar lines are all there and legible; the
  staves run diagonally from lower left to upper right and converge into the distance.
- **Ink in relief.** Note heads and beams stand slightly proud of the page and catch light; the title "Winter" (from
  "Four Seasons") is cut in tall raised letters at the start of the page.
- **Notes light up as they sound.** Each note head becomes a small LED-like light the instant it is played, in its
  staff's colour: the solo violin red, the violins cyan and magenta, the violas white, the cellos green, the continuo warm
  orange. The newest notes flare with a small cross-shaped star; played notes keep glowing and fade over a few seconds,
  so a fast passage leaves a lit trail across the staff.
- **Light falls on the paper.** Each lit note throws a soft coloured pool onto the page around it; dense passages wash
  a whole region in colour.
- **A dark, misty room.** Beyond the near page the air is blue-black; a slow mist drifts low over the paper; tiny
  bright motes hang in the air.
- **The camera rides the music.** It glides along the system, keeping the playhead in view, sometimes low and raking,
  sometimes higher, swinging between the top staves (the solo) and the whole orchestra as the music shifts.

## Kept as the style (fixed in every film)

the printed score as the world (one endless system, time = distance), ink in relief and a raised title, a light per
voice that switches on with the note and fades as a trail, coloured pools on the paper, bloom, a dark room with fog and
mist, a camera that glides with the playhead and frames what sounds, sound made from the same notes.

## Not copied

Vivaldi's notes, the source's palette assignment and its edit. Every film designs its own piece, staves, colours, room
and shots from a brief. The template is an original composition; the worked example is Bach.

## Departures, on purpose

- **Proportional spacing.** A note's x is its time (beat × U), so the camera's glide is the tempo. Engraved scores
  space by duration logarithmically; at reading distances the difference is invisible and the motion is smoother.
- **Synthesised sound.** The film needs no recording: the synth reads the same notes, so picture and sound cannot drift.
