What the clip shows: one shot split in two, stacked. Top panel labelled **before**, bottom panel **after**, white

leads, the tail and the body lag and settle, the stance has weight.

## What this skill takes
- **The form**: the same beat played twice at once, before over after, with `before` / `after` pills and a beat
  label in the after panel. A comparison anyone reads in two seconds.
- **The idea**: the principles are a *pass* on top of a first pass. The story poses do not change; what changes is
  spacing, anticipation, arcs, overlap, squash, secondary action. This skill makes the pass explicit (story keys vs
  `pass: true` keys + springs) and measurable (the meter), so other skills can use the same pass on their own motion.
- **A painterly creature** made of thick strokes — here as 2D gouache daubs in code.

## What it does not take
- Not the fox, not the snowfield, not the mousing dive, not the 3D brush-stroke renderer, not the clip's frames or
  timing. Every piece designs its own creature, place, beats and labels from its topic.
- No AI-generated motion: the skill is code-only. The before is the honest first pass of the same story keys (what a
  first pass in code looks like), not a capture of anyone's output.
