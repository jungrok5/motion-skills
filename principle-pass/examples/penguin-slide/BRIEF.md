# Belly slide — principle pass (the worked example)

## Topic
- A penguin waddles four short steps on ice, flops onto its belly and slides to a fish.
- Why: a different body plan from the starter (upright biped, flippers, flat feet, stub tail) and three different
  lessons — planted steps (the before skates), a fall that needs a gather (anticipation), a long slowing slide and a
  stop that rocks and settles (timing, follow-through).
- 7 s of action and rest, 3 s of study; 10.4 s.

## Creature
- biped, upright; small but heavy for its size (mass 1.3 — the gather 0.28 s, the flop 0.52 s, the slide 2 s).
- body 118 × 92 (pear: hip .95, belly 1.05, chest .82), neck 6, head 50 × 52 with a beak (snout .3), tail 24 × 16
  (3 segments), legs 24 / 22 / 26 with flat feet planted by the toe, flippers 42 / 34 (free).
- palette: coat #2c2e35, shade #1a1b20, light #4c505b, belly #f2efe7 (the whole front: bellyFrom −.08), beak #e08a32, feet #e39040.
- follows through: tail (f 2.4, z .35), head on a spring (f 3, z .5), squash (f 4, z .32).
- idle: breath every 2.1 s, blinks ~3.6 s apart.

## Pass
| beat | timing / spacing | anticipation | arcs | follow-through / overlap | squash & stretch | secondary | exaggeration |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| waddle | hips keyed at every contact (20 px a step), dip −3 on contact, rise +1 over the passing foot | — | each foot lifts on an arc | body rocks ±.05, flippers out | — | breath | — |
| flop | the gather holds 0.28 s, the fall is fast | lean back .2, crouch −7, flippers up and back, head up | hips hop +22 on the way down; the head swings on a wide arc | flippers lead, feet trail off the ice (release .2 s) | gather .93, stretch 1.06, belly flattens 1.14 on contact | — | lean back further than a real bird would |
| slide | impact 'skid': keeps 90 % of its forward speed, slows over 2 s (Hermite to a stop) | — | — | at the stop the body rocks forward −.09 and back, flippers flick, head nods on its spring, tail settles | 1.03 while sliding | — | — |
- Staging: side view, left → right; the fish is in frame from the start so the slide has a goal.
- Feet: LN / LF alternate (LN first): 1.05–1.25, 1.35–1.55, 1.65–1.85, 1.95–2.15, closing step 2.25–2.45; release at 2.93 / 2.96.
- Study: beak, hips, near foot from 2.4 s.

## Review
- Round 1: legs out of reach in the waddle (steps of 34 px for 46 px legs) → 20 px steps, hips keyed per contact,
  standing hips at 42 (knees bent).
- Round 2: a foot popped when it was released (the free pose put the toe elsewhere) → a foot target is always the
  contact point (toe of a flat foot), planted or free.
- Round 3: white front read as random spots (pale daubs over a black underpaint) → a belly underpaint band; zoom 1.5.
- Round 4: all 10 measured principles pass; before fails 8 (feet skate 66 px, no anticipation, no settle, frozen rests).
