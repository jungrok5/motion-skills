# Source

Made from the post by @flxrnc — https://x.com/flxrnc/status/2125681086722097551?a=cv8JTTCnpK--OKdJuUQ&s=19 — read
through `api.fxtwitter.com/2/thread/2125681086722097551` and the clip from `video.twimg.com` (CONTRIBUTING.md, "X · Threads
게시물에서 출처 읽기"). The post lists 15 GitHub repos for animation ("I animate anything I imagine now…") over a 36-second
vertical (1440 × 1800) film in which each repo is **embroidered** on a sewing table.

## What the clip does (read from the frames)
- Linen cloth, a warm low sun through leaves, sewing things scattered: spools, scissors, a thimble, a pincushion, buttons.
- For each repo, a needle with dark thread sews a satin-stitch patch (an icon of what the repo does), the name is
  stitched below letter by letter (unstitched letters faint, as if marked), and a paper tag with a dashed red border
  lands beside it (a one-line description, the GitHub handle in mono).
- One gliding, low, close camera with shallow depth of field moves from repo to repo, ~2 s each; tags sometimes above,
  sometimes below.
- At the end the camera pulls back over the whole table, a yellow thread winds through every patch, and the film ends on
  a cream card: "15 repos. Stitch by stitch." with the numbered list.

## What this skill takes
The style and the grammar: patch + embroidered title + paper tag per item, the needle that sews it, the table and its
light, the gliding camera, the overview with the thread, the end card. The structure "a list of N things, one per
beat".

## What it does not take
The repos, their names, icons, descriptions, the "15 repos" card, the clip's exact palette or layout, or any frame of it.
Nothing from the clip is in this repository. Every reel designs its own items, patches, palette and words from its topic;
the worked example 「비빔밥 한 그릇」 is a recipe, not a repo list.
