# A small morning — brief

## Topic
The starter: three things from one morning, in the order they happen. Small enough to read in one go; a shape to copy,
not a look to reuse — a new topic gets its own items, patches and palette (references/BRIEF.md).

## Items
| # | title | tag line | small line | patch idea |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coffee | first cup, slowly | kettle · 92°C | a cream cup with steam on an indigo rounded base |
| 2 | Sunrise | the window, 6:40 | east · clear sky | sun over two green hills, running-stitch clouds; sky base, navy edge (tag above) |
| 3 | Bicycle | the long way to work | 7.2 km · river path | wheels and a red frame in satin lines on a cream pill |

## Patches
Base first, picture next, details and the edge last. Bases change colour and shape (indigo rect, sky rect, cream pill).

## Palette
Cloth `#cfbe9c`, title thread `#202748`, paper `#f5eedd`, accent `#b5332b`, cord `#e2bd45`; threads indigo, cream,
sky `#93cfe9`, sun `#f5c242`, greens `#3f9a5c` `#2e7a48`, red `#d64535`.

## Timing
Defaults (beat 2.2, stitch 1.2, travel .7) → about 14 s with the outro.

## Props
Default mix.

## Stills review
From `render.mjs starter --items` at full size: every patch reads without its title; satin rows and raised edges show;
tags readable (caps ≥ 38 px); the needle rises before each plunge and stays planted; the camera frames each item whole
when its tag lands; the end card fills the frame with the three names at 36 px.
