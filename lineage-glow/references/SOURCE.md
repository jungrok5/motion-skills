## What this skill takes (the style)
- Warm light on near-black, added (overlaps brighten), with bloom, faint dust and grain.
- One subject node low in the frame with a warm pool of light under it.
- Thin S-curves that leave a node vertically and arrive vertically; a bright tip while a line grows.
- The shapes: the doubling tree, the single traced line, the convergence into one point, the burst back out.
- Compression: near rows wide apart, far rows packed into bright bands under a horizon line with haze.
- The counter: small gold tracked label + big bold number, values blurring into each other, gold at the climax.
- The timeline axis: tiny grey years on the left that move with the camera.
- The switch: lit gold ↔ cold grey nodes.
- Captions: two short lines at the top, one thought each, fading in with a little rise; drifting words that blur away.
- Calm pacing, slow eased camera, ending on one light.

## What it does not take
- The script, its argument and its claims (the gene "switch" and the self-affirmation message are the creator's,
  not checked here), the figures 1036 / 3억 / 30-year generations, the wording of any caption.
- The music.
- Any frame as a template: each film designs its own beats from its own topic (references/BRIEF.md).
