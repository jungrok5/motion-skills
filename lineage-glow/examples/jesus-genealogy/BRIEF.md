# 예수님의 족보 — BRIEF

TOPIC      "샘플 주제는 예수님 계보를 설명하는 걸로" — explain the genealogy of Jesus in this style
THREAD     A line of names kept for two thousand years comes down to one person, and from him opens to all nations.
SUBJECT    예수 — the node at the bottom (world 0,0), labelled 예수
LENGTH     102 s, 9:16, 30 fps

SHAPES
  - chain, grown DOWN: Matthew writes "A begat B" from Abraham to Jesus, so the light travels down a single line
    (the reference grew up from 나; a genealogy as written is read downward). A grey ghost shows the road ahead.
  - switch: at the exile the kings' stretch dims to grey: the kingdom is lost, but the line is not cut. Then it relights.
  - brackets: Matthew's own grouping, three sets of fourteen (1:17).
  - side nodes: the five women Matthew names, each beside the father's row with a line into the child; Joseph → Jesus
    thins while Mary's line carries the light (1:16 changes the formula).
  - chain, grown UP, packed: Luke climbs past Abraham to Adam and God; the rows pack toward a horizon (the
    reference's compression).
  - fan + converge: from Jesus, countless lines spread to all nations (Genesis 22:18's promise), then draw back in.

FACTS
- Matthew 1:2–16 names, top to bottom (개역 spellings): 아브라함 이삭 야곱 유다 베레스 헤스론 람 아미나답 나손 살몬 보아스 오벳
  이새 다윗 | 솔로몬 르호보암 아비야 아사 여호사밧 요람 웃시야 요담 아하스 히스기야 므낫세 아몬 요시야 | 여고냐 스알디엘
  스룹바벨 아비훗 엘리아김 아소르 사독 아킴 엘리웃 엘르아살 맛단 야곱 요셉 예수 (41 names).
- Matthew 1:17: fourteen generations from Abraham to David, from David to the exile, from the exile to the Christ. Read
  here as Abraham–David (14), David–Josiah (14, David counted again), Jeconiah–Jesus (14), a common reading that
  makes each set 14 with the names as listed. The counter shows each set 1–14 and steps back to 1 on David.
- The women: 다말 (1:3, mother of 베레스 by 유다), 라합 (1:5, mother of 보아스 by 살몬), 룻 (1:5, mother of 오벳 by 보아스),
  우리야의 아내 (1:6, mother of 솔로몬 by 다윗), 마리아 (1:16). Rahab was a Canaanite of Jericho (Joshua 2), Ruth a Moabite
  (Ruth 1:4), Tamar's and Bathsheba's stories are of wrong done to them (Genesis 38, 2 Samuel 11). Hence the careful
  caption "이방에서 온 사람도, 아픈 사연을 지닌 사람도", which makes no claim about any one woman.
- 1:16 changes the formula: "…마리아의 남편 요셉을 낳았으니 마리아에게서 그리스도라 칭하는 예수가 나시니라" (개역한글, public
  domain). The caption quotes the short end of it.
- Luke 3:34–38 above Abraham: 데라 나홀 스룩 르우 벨렉 헤버 살라 가이난 아박삿 셈 노아 레멕 므두셀라 에녹 야렛 마할랄렐
  가이난 에노스 셋 아담, "그 위는 하나님이시니라" (3:38). Only 노아, 아담, 하나님 are labelled.
- Dates on the axis are approximate and marked so: Abraham 기원전 약 2000년, David 기원전 약 1000년, Jeconiah taken to
  Babylon 기원전 597년 (2 Kings 24:12–15), Jesus' birth 기원전 약 4년.
- The promise: Genesis 22:18 "네 씨로 말미암아 천하 만민이 복을 얻으리니"; Galatians 3:8, 16 reads it of Christ. The
  caption paraphrases ("아브라함에게 하신 약속은 이 한 분에게서 이루어지고").
QUOTES     only 마 1:16's last clause, 개역한글 (public domain since 2012). Everything else is paraphrase.
LOOK       standard palette. Named women and group counts in gold. The ghost road is the "off" grey.
SOUND      D major; a bell per name rising through the scale within each set of fourteen; Bm + air for the exile; F#m
           and shimmer for Luke; riser → hit into the burst; resolve on D.
