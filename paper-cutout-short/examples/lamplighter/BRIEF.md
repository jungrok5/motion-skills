# BRIEF — The Lamplighter

Topic: "a fox who lights the lamps of a harbour town at dusk" (chosen as the proving topic: not in the source clip, and it needs
things the source does not have — a light that changes over the film, a prop that must touch a target, a lighthouse, the sea).

- **Hero puppet** — Ember, a fox lamplighter. Rust-orange head with a cream jaw and a long snout, tall ears, a navy coat that
  flares below the hips (it hides the top of her legs), brass buttons on the front edge, a flat cap with a brass band, a
  bushy tail with a white tip. Navy + rust read apart at any size; brass is her accent. She carries a lighting pole with a
  small flame at the tip.
- **Others** — a sailboat out at sea that comes home at the end (a prop, rocking between the wave strips).
- **The set** — one harbour quay seen side-on: a dusk sheet (blue → violet → peach at the horizon) that cross-fades to a night
  sheet; clouds hanging on threads; the sun lowered on a thread behind the sea; the moon pushed up on a wooden stick; stars let
  down on threads; a far headland with tiny houses; three sea strips that slide against each other; four harbour houses with
  scalloped roofs on the left; the cobbled quay with a stone wall; two street lamps; a striped lighthouse at the end of the pier;
  out-of-focus mooring posts and rope in front.
- **Light** — dusk going to night over the first 12 s. Warm light comes only from what Ember lights: lamp 1 → its houses'
  windows one by one → lamp 2 → the lighthouse, whose vellum beam starts to sweep.
- **The action** —
  1. Ember walks in along the quay, pole over her shoulder (0–3.4 s).
  2. She stops under lamp 1, raises the pole, the flame touches the glass: the lamp lights, then the windows behind light up.
  3. She hurries on to lamp 2 and lights it as the moon comes up (to 9.6 s).
  4. At the foot of the lighthouse she lights a little lantern on a hook; it is hauled up the tower on a thread; the lamp room
     lights and the beam begins to turn (9.6–14.2 s).
  5. Night: the boat comes home; Ember signals it with her pole, turns over and walks home along the lit quay (14.2–20.2 s).
- **The gag / the turn** — the lantern climbing the tower on a visible thread (the stage's strings are part of the charm).
- **Camera** — one long truck along the quay (9.6 s) → card wipe → a close shot that tilts up the lighthouse with the lantern
  (4.6 s) → dissolve → the wide night harbour (6 s). 20.2 s.
- **Parts** — all cut in code (cast.js, the set in index.html), so the example runs offline.
- **Title label** — "The Lamplighter" · "paper harbour · No. 1 · 12 fps", pinned on at 15.8 s.
- **Sound** — a music-box waltz in F over surf; paper taps on her steps; a flame catching at each lamp; the pulley creaks as the
  lantern climbs; a chime and a harmonium chord when the lighthouse lights; a small bell from the boat.
