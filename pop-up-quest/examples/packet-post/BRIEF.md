# BRIEF — The Packet Post

## 1. The topic and the journey
- **Topic**: what happens between clicking a link and seeing the page — DNS, the TCP handshake, the HTTP request,
  packets, rendering.
- **Reader**: curious non-programmers, students meeting networking for the first time.
- **The errand**: Quill, a pigeon courier, carries one request for the page at harbor.town from the first page to the
  last, and comes home with an empty satchel when the page is in the window.
- **Chapters**:

| # | place (landmark) | idea | sky | prop | task | when solved |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Address Tower (stone drum, reading room) | DNS: a name becomes an IP address | morning | address book on a lectern | choice | a banner with 93.184.216.34 unrolls down the tower |
| 2 | The Knocking Ferry (two docks across a river) | TCP three-way handshake | day | dock bell | order (3) | three lanterns light on the far bank, the raft poles across |
| 3 | The Request Post Office (columns, pediment, roof tube) | HTTP request: method + path | day | red postbox | choice | a "200 OK" envelope flies out of the roof tube to Quill |
| 4 | The Sorting Loom (timber frame, belt, numbered parcels) | packets and sequence numbers | dusk | the loom's crank | count | four parcels stack in order 1–4 |
| 5 | The Window House (one great four-pane window) | rendering: parse, style, layout, paint | night | lamplighter's pole | order (4) | the panes light one by one, the room glows |
