# BRIEF — The Long Way Down

## 1. The topic and the journey
- **Topic**: the water cycle — evaporation, condensation, precipitation, collection.
- **Reader**: children of 8–11 and anyone who half-remembers it from school.
- **The errand**: Drizzle, one raindrop, makes the whole round trip: off the sea, into a cloud, down as snow, home by river.
- **Chapters**:

| # | place (landmark) | idea | sky | prop | task | when solved |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Sun Pans (salt trays on a beach, a bay) | evaporation leaves salt behind | morning | Reed's brass mirror | choice | vapour wisps curl up from the bay |
| 2 | The Cloud Loft (a hut on stilts under mountains) | condensation | day | cold-air bellows | order (4) | three paper clouds swell |
| 3 | The Snow Ridge (weather post, snowy pines) | precipitation: snow below 0 °C | day | thermometer board | choice | snowflakes drop on threads from the heavy cloud |
| 4 | The River Mouth (water mill, the river widening to the sea) | collection, back to the ocean | dusk | sluice wheel | choice | the gate lifts, the mill wheel speeds up, the boat sails out |

## 3–5. Words, look, tour
Title "The Long Way Down" / "A RAINDROP’S ROUND TRIP". Cover plum `#6a3f63`, cloth slate `#3f5666`, accent gold.
Ground: sand and bay (1), green hills (2), snowfield (3), a river widening to the sea at dusk (4). 4 × 8.6 s, 37.7 s.

## Review
The heron reads as a heron only with the long beak and plume (a plain `bird` head read as a pigeon); the raindrop's
point leans back so the face still turns right. Wading in the bay is the heron's place, so Reed stands in the water
on purpose.
