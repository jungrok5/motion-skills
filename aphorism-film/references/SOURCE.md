## The style, distilled (what is fixed in this skill)
- **Type** — white editorial type stacked in the left third: tiny tracked caps, a huge high-contrast Didone word, italic
  connectors, sometimes a bold grotesk word or condensed caps; a tiny attribution column. Words arrive with a rise or a
  motion smear; the stack holds through camera moves.
- **Pictures** — flat colour, one cel shadow, ink outlines, maximal pattern (damask, tiles, posters, papers, foliage,
  flowers); vermilion, cobalt, emerald, cream, black, saffron, pink; anime figures with blunt bangs and colour streaks.
- **Scale** — a figure tiny in a big architectural space, or a close-up face staring out; nothing in between for long.
- **Transitions as events** — paper storms, walls that crack and fall, a paper vortex, an ink flood.
- **The loop** — the last image returns to the first.

## What is NOT taken
The quotes, the eye mural, the girls' designs, the pistol, the telephone, the river room, the music — none of it. Each
topic gets its own lines (QUOTES.md), scenes, cast and score. The worked example (`examples/time`) is about time — a
subject the video never touches — and its starter film is about beginnings and knowledge.
