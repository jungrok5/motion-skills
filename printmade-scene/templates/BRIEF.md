# 문 닫은 이발소 — brief (starter)

## Topic
A granddaughter goes back into her grandfather's barbershop, closed for years. Mood: quiet family mystery — tender,
a little uncanny. Label 「이발소」, caption 「가족 미스터리 · 문 닫은 이발소」. 13.4 s.

## Place
One room in the folded view: the back wall face on (sage-grey stripe paper over a dark wainscot, a big mirror over a
counter of bottles, a barber pole, a pendulum clock above the door, a rain window on the right with a curtain), the
floor laid out flat below it (a check of cream and slate tiles, rows parallel to the frame), the barber chair standing
in the middle at G 800 with its seat and back in elevation, a pendant lamp above it. The wall meets the floor at y 610.
Palette now: slate, sage-grey, dark umber, dusty rose on the pole. Accent: crimson — only the cape.

## Two printings
- **A, now** — a rainy night. Cool slate grade; the pole's stripes faded to dusty rose; the clock stopped; a note on a
  slip by the window: 「아직도 그 냄새가 난다」; rain come in under the window; the lamp buzzes (flickering cone).
- **B, then** — the same block printed warm: a sunny afternoon, straw wallpaper, the pole's stripes crimson, the
  pendulum swinging, the morning's cut hair around the chair, a hat and coat on the rack.
- **The change when A returns** — the crimson cape lies over the chair (it was not there before). It prints in from the
  top as the roller passes back.
