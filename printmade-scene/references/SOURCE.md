# Source

**전혜정 (@goevel), 「인디 게임을 만들자」 — brunch, 14 Feb 2016 — https://brunch.co.kr/@goevel/3**

A one-person company's diary of making 「마마」, a family horror-mystery adventure adapted from her own film script: a
mother overly attached to her son since her husband's death, a dead father's diary, a family secret in a mansion. The
post covers the scenario, the puzzle and level-design problems of turning a film into a game, the art, development in
Unity and later Unreal, the music by 미확인소음물체, and the team.

## What this skill takes
- **The look of the concept art** (by 김도윤): a 판화 (printmaking) style — flat inks, grain, scratched hatching — with
  the 동양화 (East Asian painting) folded perspective: walls face on, floors laid flat, a rug that doesn't agree with the
  room's perspective; tall thin people with long faces and flushed cheeks; a vertical room name in the corner and a
  small title line at the foot. See references/LOOK.md.
- **The post's lesson.** They wanted NPR and tried to keep the art's look in 3D by cutting the artist's plates into pieces
  and mapping them onto models by hand; the post calls the result a failure and notes that 3D tools can't do the
  distorted perspective. So this skill stays 2D and draws the folded view directly, animating only what lives in it.
- **The mechanic as a form.** In the game, right-clicking shows a room's past, where the hints are. Here every scene is
  the present plate, re-printed as the past (or another state) by an ink roller, then the present again — changed.

## What it does not take
None of the plates, characters (진우, his mother, the aunt, the burned girl), rooms (the mansion, its pool, the bathroom
with the glass wall), the game's story, its in-engine screenshots, its music, or the title 「마마」. The starter and the
example are new topics (a barbershop, a schoolyard) designed from briefs; the kit's crimson vine is a generic tool to be
used only when a vine *is* the clue.
