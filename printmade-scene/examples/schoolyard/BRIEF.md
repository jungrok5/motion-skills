# 폐교 운동장 — brief (worked example)

## Topic
A father comes back, in the rain, to the playground of a school that has closed — the one his daughter went to.
Mood: family mystery, grief held in. Label 「운동장」, caption 「가족 미스터리 · 폐교」. 14 s. Not in the starter:
an exterior, a child, a prop held all the way through (the umbrella), a thrown object, a front layer (the fence).

## Place
An exterior in the folded view, laid out like the source's mansion plate: the sky and a two-storey brick school stand
face on along the top (elevation, wall line y 470), the playground is laid out flat below (plan): beaten earth, the
white running track as a flat oval, puddles. The swing frame stands at G 760 in elevation, its swings hanging. Black
bare trees at the sides. A low iron fence with a gap runs across the very front, drawn after the figures (front layer).
Palette now: dusk violet-grey, slate, wet earth, black trees. Accent: crimson — only the ball.

## Two printings
- **A, now** — dusk, rain across the whole plate (white scratches), the windows dark, some boarded with planks, the
  gable clock stopped at ten past four, puddles rippling, the swings almost still; a slip on the right: 「여기서 기다릴게」.
- **B, then** — a summer afternoon from the same block: pale sky, cream brick, lit windows with curtains, leafy trees,
  pots by the wall, the clock running, one swing still moving.
- **The change when A returns** — the red ball lies under the left swing, where it rolled that afternoon.
