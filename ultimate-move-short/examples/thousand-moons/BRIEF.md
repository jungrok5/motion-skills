# BRIEF — 「月蝕 · Thousand-Moon Eclipse」

Topic given: "a spear fighter whose ultimate is a thousand moons" (a new topic — not in the reference clip).

## The fighter
- **Who**: a young woman, a wandering spear-bearer. Calm, almost sleepy eyes; never shouts.
- **Silhouette**: black high-collared coat cut short in front, long ragged tails behind; a white sash with two long
  tails; a high black ponytail tied with a white cord (the second white shape that reads at any size).
- **Weapon**: a straight spear (yari), 2.2 m, held in both hands; the left hand slides along the shaft.
- **Build**: slight (scale .96), soft jaw, lashes; the head a little large (anime proportion).
- **Accent**: blood red (`#e0142d`) — the eclipse colour. Only in: her eyes when the power wakes, the hairline inside the
  ink, the edge of the spearhead, the threads between moons, the cut. Nothing else is red.

## Her powers (the topic's own names)
1. **月影步 Moon-shadow step** — every fast move drags a wet ink shadow behind her, curved like a waning crescent;
   the faster, the sharper its edge, with red hairlines inside.
2. **弦月痕 Crescent marks** — every strike leaves an unseen crescent in the air. Two crossing strikes wake one for a
   moment: a tiny moon that waxes full and goes out (the first omen).
3. **Ultimate — 千月蝕 · 無光一閃 Thousand-Moon Eclipse · the lightless flash** — a thousand moons wax full in every
   depth of the sky, all turned toward her; threads of red tie them. They crack like black glass, the shards hang, then
   pour into the spearhead; the last giant moon is eclipsed (dark disc, red corona) and closes. One horizontal sweep:
   the sky is cut along a red hairline and the two halves of the world slide apart.

## World
- A white salt flat, a pale grey sky, bare trees dissolving in the haze on the horizon (`far: 'trees'`).
- Cracks her steps leave stay on the ground for the whole film.

## Sound
Percussion at 176 BPM under 0–15 s (kick/snare/metal), a dash or a slash on every move, a land on every landing. 15 s:
the kit stops, a heartbeat, low drone. 18 s: glass shimmer and a low resonance as the moons open. 22 s: cracks, glass,
riser; drums gone. 25 s: near-silence (0.3 s), then the ultimate hit. 28 s: a ring, cloth, the last wisp; nothing else.

## QUALITY STANDARD (score every still against this)
- Black, white and one red. The red is a hairline, never a fill (the burst frame is the one exception).
- The fighter reads in silhouette at any size: the ponytail, the sash tails and the spear are always clear of the body.
- Ink is wet brushwork — streaks along the motion, torn blotted edges, flecks — never flat vector shapes or smoke.
- Every effect has a cause: a move, a strike or a landing. No random sparks.
- Fast and still alternate: dense action to 15 s, a real stillness 15–18 s, the domain growing 18–22 s, the cut 25 s.
- Bodies: knees bend forward, the head and chest face the same way, feet touch the ground, she moves where she faces.

## MUST NOT LOOK LIKE
- The reference: no black-haired swordsman, no eyes, no katana. Moons, a spear and a ponytail.
- A 3D game, a particle demo, smoke, lightning, fire, neon, more than one accent colour.
