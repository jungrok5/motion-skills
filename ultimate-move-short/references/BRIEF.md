## Review rubric — score every still (and the contact sheet) before rendering
Run `render.mjs film.html --sheet 32 --scale .5 --q mb=1`, then ~10 full-size stills (`--stills a,b,c`) at moments that
are NOT shot starts. A still fails on any of these:

**Colour**
1. More than black, white, greys and the one accent. The accent is a hairline, a glint or an eye — never a fill
   (the single burst impact frame is the only exception).

**Ink**
2. Ink reads as vector shapes — hard outlines, uniform-width lines with round caps, parallel lines along an edge,
   flat cut-out silhouettes. It must be wet brushwork: a dense body, edges that break into tapered streaks along the
   motion, blots, flecks.
3. Ink appears without a cause (no move, strike or landing made it), or covers the fighter's head and chest.
4. A trail fills the frame because the camera passed through it (use `near`, or end the trail before the overtake).

**Body** (CONTRIBUTING.md — the cast sheet `examples/cast` and check.mjs `body` must pass)
5. "Did the neck turn or the torso?" — head, chest, hips and toes must agree; the near shoulder is in front.
6. A knee bends backwards, a hand floats off the hilt, a foot sinks or floats, she moves where she is not facing
   (unless the key says `back: 1`).
7. The weapon reads as a lightsaber: the accent must stay a thin line on the edge; the steel stays visible.

**Grammar**
8. The first frame is not already in motion.
9. No real stillness: 15–18 s (scaled) must slow to almost nothing — debris hangs, sound drops.
10. The domain does not grow: one → three → a thousand; the figure must become tiny against it at least once.
11. The cut is not one stroke across the whole world, or the world does not visibly slide along it.
12. The end is not quiet: white, a figure, a closing seam, one wisp. No explosion in the last 2 s.

**Frame**
13. The fighter is cut off at the edge of a shot that is about the fighter; a key element (the omen, the eclipse)
    sits outside the frame.
14. Motion-blur strobing (visible duplicate copies) on a fast camera move — lower that shot's `mb` there.
15. Cracks, rings or shards from an earlier shot pile up in a later one (`until` on every crack).
