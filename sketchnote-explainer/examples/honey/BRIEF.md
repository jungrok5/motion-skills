# BRIEF — how honey is made

**Topic (as the user said it):** "꿀은 어떻게 만들어져? 스케치 설명 영상으로"
**What the viewer should understand at the end (one sentence):** honey is flower nectar that bees carry home, change with
an enzyme, dry with their wings until it is under ~18 % water, and seal with wax — and one bee makes very little of it.
**Facts checked against:** NC State University News, "How do bees make honey?" (2013); Mississippi State University
Extension, "How honey is made"; Maine DACF "Fun Bee Facts" (≈ 1/12 teaspoon per worker, an often-quoted average).
Nectar is commonly up to ~80 % water (it varies a lot by flower); ripe honey is ~17–18 % water (standards: under 18.6–20 %);
invertase splits sucrose into glucose + fructose; bees fan the comb to evaporate water, then cap ripe cells with wax.
