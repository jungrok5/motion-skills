# LOOK — what makes it read as the reference

Studied frame by frame from the reference clip (Lemomo's "iPad Sketch Explainer", 「From Cherry to Cup」, 51 s, 1920×1080,
https://x.com/lemomo_ai/status/2117670248275186162). These are the things that, when missing, make it look like a
different style.

## The sheet
- **One sheet, never wiped.** Everything drawn stays; the camera moves over it. The last frame is the whole sheet at 1×.
- **Paper:** warm cream (`#f1ebe0`), a faint warm vignette, fine grain over everything (also over the subtitle card).
- **Plenty of empty paper.** Drawings are islands with room around them; the finished sheet is about half ink.

## The hand
- **Order inside a drawing:** faint grey construction lines (a centre line, an ellipse for a rim, a crosshair through a
  section) → the dark line → the colour → notes. The guides stay, very faint.
- **Line:** dark warm brown (`#3a302a`), not black; a little thicker where pressed, tapered at the ends, a second lighter
  pass a hair off it. Closed shapes run a little past where they started.
- **Colour:** flat watercolour, muted (sage, tomato, mustard, cinnamon, coffee, sky), **set a few px down-right of the
  line** like a misregistered print, with a slightly darker pooled edge. Never a gradient, never a colour outline.
- **Colour comes in after the line**, as a quick wipe; the stylus scribbles while it does.

## Words
- **Labels** are handwriting (Caveat-like) in the line colour, with a smaller second-language line under them (Gaegu for
  Korean; the reference had Chinese). Keep them short: "2 seeds inside / 两粒种子".
- **Red** (`#e0533f`) is for arrows, temperatures and numbers ("~196 °C"), heat squiggles and the title's underline.
- **The title** is written last, big, with a yellow highlighter swash behind the key words and a red underline; a smaller
  second-language line under it.
- **The note card** is a sage rectangle with a double edge, top right, in handwriting.

## The stylus
- A white Apple-Pencil-like stylus, tip at the drawing point, body down-right, a soft shadow on the paper that moves away
  when it lifts. It touches every stroke; between strokes it hops; between beats it slides out of the frame.

## The camera
- **Close on one drawing per beat** (≈ 1.8–2× on a 1920 sheet), the drawing above the subtitle card.
- **Glides** between beats (ease in/out, about a second), breathing out a little on long moves; a very slow push-in while
  a beat holds. The finale pulls back to the whole sheet over ~2 s.

## The subtitle card
- Bottom left, an off-white rounded card with a soft shadow; the main line in a clean sans (the reference: Chinese), the
  second line in handwriting (English). Fades in and out with each beat.

## Sound
- The reference has a steady soft music bed. This skill adds a felt-piano bed (slow, warm), a quiet chime on each beat and
  the pencil itself — a dry scratch per line or word, a softer swish while colour goes in.
