## Load path
Guyed masts: each segment's carbon beam is in compression; two tendons down its back over a spreader take the
bending; a hydraulic cylinder across each joint holds the angle. The legs hang from abduction blocks on fore-aft pins
between lugs at the ends of two transverse girders under the keel (front x 3.3, rear x −3.5).
