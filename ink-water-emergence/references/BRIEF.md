# Brief — template and review rubric

Write this into the project as `BRIEF.md` before touching code. Fill every field for THIS topic; the starter's answers
(a guardian lion) are an example, not defaults.

```
TITLE:        「…」 (a short title; a second language line is welcome)
SUBJECT:      one line — what condenses out of the ink and what it does (refs.mjs reads this line)
CREATURE:     body kind (quadruped / swimmer / a new kind built in the project), size in metres, proportions,
              signature parts (mane, horns, fins, flukes, crest …), markings, eyes, what makes it THIS animal
LOOK:         ink densities — what stays pale (flanks, belly), what is dark (mane, back, claws), what smokes most (emit),
              what condenses first (sweep: head / tail / centre)
EMERGENCE:    the e curve — when it is only wisps, a translucent pre-form (e ≈ .2–.4), readable (≈ .6), full ink (≈ 1);
              does it let go again at the end?
SETTING:      studio tank with a soft floor, or open water (no floor); eddy size (scale) and texture size (detail) for
              this animal's size; paper tone
ACTION:       the movement per stage (walks, stops, crouches, roars / glides, sweeps a fin, rises …)
SHOTS:        5–8 rows: name · seconds · framing (wide / medium, never tighter than head-and-mane) · camera drift ·
              what forms or happens · fluid
SOUND:        the water (drip, air, swell, ink blooms), the music (drone + a slow line), the creature's voice, the hit
              into the hold
QUALITY STANDARD:
  - the ink moves like ink in water: billows, curls, filaments; never blur, never hairy vertical streaks
  - the pre-form suggests the shape before you can read it; the shape stays consistent from cut to cut
  - the formed creature is a pale wash with dark pools and wet edges, and keeps smoking at the edges
  - every body is possible from every side (cast sheet clean, check.mjs ok)
  - high-key paper-grey frame, warm-grey thin ink, near-black thick ink
MUST NOT LOOK LIKE:
  - a vector cartoon (hard outlines, flat fills, round "cute" faces)
  - a particle effect or glowing smoke; colour; text on screen
  - a static drawing with a blur filter; a creature that pops in without the pre-form
```

## Review rubric (score every sheet and ~8 random full stills; 2 = yes, 1 = partly, 0 = no)
| # | question |
|---|---|
| 1 | Does the ink read as ink in water — curling billows and fine filaments, a soft upward drift? |
| 2 | In the pre-form stills, is the shape suggested but not yet readable? |
| 3 | Once formed, is the creature readable in silhouette, eyes / face in the right place, from this camera? |
| 4 | Does the formed body look painted (pale wash, dark pools, wet edges, rim) rather than CG or vector? |
| 5 | Is it still fluid at full form (edges smoking, a little bend)? |
| 6 | Body check: head, chest, hips and feet one way; near legs over the body; knees/hocks bend correctly; paws on the floor? (A still that makes you ask "is the neck or the body turned?" fails.) |
| 7 | Is the framing medium or wider, with the part that is forming in frame and the camera drifting? |
| 8 | Is the frame high-key with deep blacks, no colour, no text? |
Anything scoring 0 is fixed before rendering the MP4; aim for ≥ 13 / 16.
