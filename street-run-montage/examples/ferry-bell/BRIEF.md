# BRIEF — The Ferry Bell

**Topic**: "a girl and her corgi racing through a seaside town to catch the last ferry" (chosen to prove the skill on a
story that is in neither the source clip nor the template).
**The errand**: the last ferry of the afternoon rings its bell down at the harbour; Hana has to get from the top of the
hill to the terminal before it leaves → the last shot is the terminal plaza with the ferry behind her.

## Cast
- **Rider**: Hana, 14, light and quick, height 1.55 m, mass 44 kg, regular stance; black bob; navy cap, logo "UMI";
  mint oversized windbreaker-hoodie, cream lining, print "SEA"; navy shorts; cream sneakers with a yellow accent;
  board: pale wood deck, mint wheels, navy / mint / yellow graphic. Signature: the mint block and the navy cap.
  Idle: weight on the back foot, breathing hard after the sprint.
- **Companion**: Mochi, a corgi: orange coat, cream belly, socks and tail tip, big ears, a red collar; 12 kg on short
  legs — quick steps rather than long strides, a long gather before it leaps. Runs on Hana's right, takes the sea side on
  the harbour, barks after the big jump, sits at the end and barks again.

## Place
Spring, afternoon (clear, cherry trees, petals in the air). Route (~240 m):
1. Top of the hill — a back lane (block walls, hedges, houses, poles and wires, a traffic mirror), 52 m down a 4.5 m slope.
2. The bend right into the shopping street — "SHIOKAZE BAKERY", "UMINEKO BOOKS", "MINATO MART", awnings, vending machines.
3. The harbour front — wide paving, bollards, lamps, the sea on the left; the seawall ledge for the grind.
4. The ferry terminal plaza — "FERRY TERMINAL", planters, bollards on the quay, the white-and-navy ferry moored.
