# SOURCE — what this skill was made from

**The post:** Chetaslua (@chetaslua) on X — https://x.com/chetaslua/status/2122095074968469720?a=WMOtOJajKg-tokfUuC4&s=19 —
"hand painted film with pure code: zero images / zero libraries / zero assets; every frame is canvas code, every sound
is Web Audio code". It quotes Vamsi Batchu's post (the reference-image method behind `storybook-portrait`) and shows a
52 s 16:9 film in that picture-book style: a girl with a puffin on her bucket hat on a sea cliff with a lighthouse
(close-up) → a painted spread of a Roman square with a cat walking, the paper showing at its ragged edges → two friends
in big coats walking through an autumn park, scarf blowing → three passengers on a tram bench in winter, snow outside →
back to the girl. Birds fly, leaves fall, figures are cut-out puppets (rigid coats, swinging legs), the sound is
synthesised. (Watched 2026-11-16 from the post's video; not kept.)

## What this skill takes
- the **method**: a whole film — pictures and sound — written as code, no image, audio or library files;
- the **form**: a few shots of a small day, picture-book spreads with crayon skies and walls, gouache forms, dry-brush hair,
  cut-out characters with big heads, bell coats and thin legs, a close-up to open and close, something alive in every
  shot, soft synthesised music and foley;
- the **painted-spread vignette** (the paper coming in over the corners), as an option per shot.

## What it does not take
- the film's shots, places or characters: no puffin, no lighthouse cliff, no Roman square, no autumn park walk, no tram.
  The starter (a windy ridge) and the example (a summer market and canal) are this repo's own;
- the reference illustration behind it (see `storybook-portrait/references/SOURCE.md`; the look's original is
  @Artedeingenio — credit them when sharing);
- any claim about how that film was coded: everything here is this repo's own engine.
