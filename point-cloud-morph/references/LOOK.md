# The look — observed from the source

Source: https://x.com/askwhykartik/status/2119452450583097368 (posted 2026-11-08). The post is a 21.9 s reel of
AI-built landing pages; **0–8 s** is the section this skill is made from: a dark site for an architecture-scanning studio
whose hero is a point cloud that changes shape as you scroll. Stills were read at 3 fps; what follows is the grammar, not
the content. (The reel's other pages — a space scroll, a solar-house site, a bag, a balm — are not this style.)

## The cloud
- **One material.** Every form is the same white points: a building, a tower, a roof truss, a terrain grid. Nothing is
  ever cut away; one form becomes the next.
- **Edges carry the shape.** Points crowd along edges (slabs, columns, mullions, truss members) into fuzzy bright strands;
  faces get a thin scatter; a few points fly off as LiDAR spray. The forms read as structure, not as solids.
- **A floor of specks.** Objects sit on a sparse dust plane that fades into the dark; terrain is drawn as rows of points
  (scan lines), so the rows themselves show the relief.
- **One cold accent.** Ice blue appears only as glowing clusters (a thermal bridge, a sensor, a partition) and a highlighted
  member. Everything else is warm white on near black.
- **Depth by brightness.** Far points dim; dense overlaps glow (additive). No lighting, no shading, no outlines.

## The morph
- The settled form **lifts into a nebula** — a turbulent, glowing cloud with a bluish core — and **settles into the next
  form**, the new shape condensing out of the cloud. About 1.5–3 s.
- The morph has a direction: parts of the new form appear before others (bottom up, one side first).
- Between big changes the cloud may also flow straight from one form into a related one (building → building) with little
  turbulence.

## The camera
- Always moving: a slow orbit (the readout ticks YAW 015 → 102 over the section), pitch 30–40°.
- The form sits right of centre when the copy is on screen, centred when the page goes immersive.
- Drag-to-orbit is offered ("DRAG TO ORBIT / ARROWS TO NAVIGATE").

## The HUD
- **Mono caps, wide tracking** for every label: `FIG. 01 / HALLMARK LIBRARY / POINT CLOUD` with a grey second line
  (`LOCAL GRID / 1 : 400`) top left; `YAW 028 / PITCH 40` with `SCAN 01 / 000%` top right.
- **Callout tags** on the model: a small marker, a thin leader, a dark tag — `FL-01 THERMAL BRIDGE · 1.9 W/m²K`.
- **3D overlays**: a dotted arc (a drone path), a tilted ring with tick marks around the terrain, thin frame lines.
- **Site chrome** when "on the page": a top bar (brand left, nav with superscript numbers, CONTACT box), a left ruler with
  numbered ticks, a headline block — kicker `(01) CAPTURE / PROJECT / LIVE LIDAR`, a two-line headline (white, then grey:
  "The ground truth / for every building."), a short body, a light button with an accent arrow box, an underlined link —
  and a bottom strip: stats (`POINTS CAPTURED 318 420 000`, `COVERAGE 100.0%`, `FLOOR AREA 9 400 M²`) and section tabs
  (01 CAPTURE · 02 ENERGY · 03 LIVING · 04 ARCHIVE) with the current one lit.
- **Immersive** shots drop the chrome and the copy; the label, readout and hint stay.

## What this skill adds
A time-driven film (MP4 + loop) as well as the scroll page; a form builder that makes any subject, not only buildings;
transition styles beyond the nebula (flow, burst, fold, scan-in); moving parts inside a form; a score; checks.
