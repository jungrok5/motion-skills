# Tundra camp

Topic (the user's words): "a reindeer herders' camp by a frozen river, late winter" — a topic Frostwind's README offers
as a prompt ("a tundra world with a frozen river, a reindeer herd and a herders' camp") but does not ship.
One sentence: bright cold morning, long blue shadows, smoke leaning in the wind, a herder walking out to the herd.

## Place and light
latitude: 69.0° N (SOURCE: Kautokeino, inland Finnmark)
season → declination: mid-March, −2°   time → 10:30 solar, hour −1.5
derived (check.mjs): sun altitude 17.4°, azimuth 156°, diffuse 0.33 → shadows fall north-north-west, 3.2 × height

wind: toward 235° (from the north-east) at 5 m/s, TASTE — streams the smoke and the blown snow the same way.

## Land and water
relief 1.2 m on a 38 m noise; a river meanders west → east (width 13 m, 1.4 m deep, frozen) in a shallow valley.
ground: wind-scoured ice where the fine noise is high, snow on the rest of the river; packed snow on the trails;
sedge tussocks show through scoured snow on high ground; snow everywhere else.

## Life and culture
flora: mountain birch 4.6 m (3–6 m at the tree line), bare, denser where moister; willow scrub 1 m on the banks.
place: the camp — two lavvus (5.2 m and 4.2 m across, 4.2 / 3.5 m tall), a woodpile, a drying rack hung with hides,
a hearth. Siting: dry, 4–14 m above the river bank (water, fishing, out of the drift), nearest 8 m from the shore.
trails: camp → east along the bank, camp → north-west to the second lavvu.
