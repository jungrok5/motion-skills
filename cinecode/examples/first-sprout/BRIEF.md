# Brief: First Sprout

The topic, as given: **"a seed sprouting, short, for a vertical feed; no words."** None of the upstream films is about
growth or a plant, and none is cut paper in cross-section, so this is the topic the absorption was proved on
(`references/BRIEF.md`, CONTRIBUTING step 6).

## Interpretations (the ★ first)

1. ★ **One drop's night.** A single mung bean, a single drop of water, one night underground told in cut paper: the drop
   lands, the bean splits, the root dives, the hooked stem shoulders through the crust, and two leaves open into morning
   light. Why: the most expected film is a time-lapse of a whole tray greening; one bean and one drop keeps the scale
   intimate and gives the film a cause (the drop) and a payoff (the dew).
2. A window-sill race: three beans, three pots, one wins the light. (A story, but a crowd of near-identical subjects.)
3. The sprout seen from the soil's side only, never the sky. (Strong form, but the light never pays off.)

## What is fixed (the kit) and what was designed here

- Fixed: Quick mode, one `film.html`, frames from time, sound in the same file, stills before a draft, the hard rules.
- Designed: the subject (a mung bean: green coat, pale scar, white root with hairs, hooked hypocotyl, two thick seed leaves,
  first true leaves), the places (a predawn windowsill pot; the soil in section as torn paper strata; the same pot at first
  light), the palette, the camera deck, the cues on an 80 BPM grid, the score, the contact sounds, the signature (the drop
  returns as dew and catches the light).

## Production card (Quick)

- **Logline:** a drop of water wakes a bean; by morning it has pushed two leaves into the light.
- **Curiosity:** where does the drop go? Held under the soil from 3 s; paid at 10–11 s when the leaves open and the dew glints.
- **Beats** (4, one action each, a bar each at 80 BPM):

  | t | beat | action | camera | in | sound |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 0–3 | hook | a drop already falling lands (0.75 s) and soaks in | tilt down + slow dolly in | — | tap on the land, one bell note |
  | 3–6 | change | the bean swells, cracks (3.75 s), the root dives | crane down after the root tip, dolly out | match on the wet front | crack tap, low pad, slow heartbeat |
  | 6–9 | turn | the hooked stem rises and breaks the crust (8.25 s) | crane up + dolly out, root to surface | cut | crust step + crumble |
  | 9–12 | payoff | the hook springs straight, the coat drops (9.75 s), leaves open, dew glints (11.25 s) | push in on the leaves | dissolve 0.5 s | swell into the morning, pad opens, lead climbs, a tick on the glint |

- **Key moments:** 0.75 s the drop lands · 3.75 s the coat cracks · 8.25 s the crust breaks · 11.25 s the dew catches the light.
- **Code fit:** the bean, root and stem are cut-paper shapes with a short shadow under each edge, seen side-on; the soil is torn
  strata with pebbles; the pot rim is a terracotta strip in front. Nothing realistic is needed; no characters, so no body checks.
- **Look:** cut paper (`papercut-collage` family: paper texture, held 12 drawings a second), own palette.
- **Palette:** predawn wall `#2c374b` · soil `#3b2a20` / `#4f3826` · sprout `#efe6c8` · coat `#4c7a3c` · leaf `#93bb47` · morning `#f3d38e`.
- **Atmosphere:** the pot before dawn: cold blue wall, one paler sheet where the window is · the soil: strata of browns, no
  sky, the water's front darkens it as it comes down · the pot at first light: the wall warms and a window's light slides in.
- **Light arc:** cold and dark for 9 s (the night happens underground), warm from 9 s; the warmth is the payoff.
- **Camera:** flat planes (`CAM`, `plane()`), calm and held; moves never repeat across adjacent shots (tilt → crane down →
  crane up → push in); signature: the drop.
- **Motion:** the drop accelerates and stretches; growth eases out (alive, never linear); the hook straightens with a small
  overshoot; the coat halves fall with gravity; leaves open with an overshoot.
- **Tempo:** standard, 3 s a shot, cuts on the bar (80 BPM: a bar is 3 s, every key moment on a beat).
- **Music (code, the brief's choice):** D major pentatonic; a lone bell for the drop; pad Dadd9 → Bm7 and a two-a-bar
  heartbeat underground; Gmaj7 → D open and a soft lead F♯5 A5 B5 D6 in the morning.
- **Sound:** contact only: the drop's tap, the coat's crack, the crust giving, one swell into the morning, the dew's tick;
  a small-room bed (a windowsill, not a hall), air in the morning.
- **Words:** none. **Finishing:** none (motion blur, print tone and gate weave off).
- **Ending:** hold on the open leaves in warm light, the dew still on the leaf.
- **Checks:** 0 s the drop reads at phone size · 3.75 s the crack reads as a split, not a face · 8.25 s the crust sits on
  the surface and lifts · 11.25 s the glint lands on the beat.
