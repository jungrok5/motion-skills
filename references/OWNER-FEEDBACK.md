# The owner's feedback, kept (every skill)

The owner's notes that hold for every skill in this repository, kept as standing rules so no session has to hear them
twice. **Read this before working on any skill.** A note about one skill goes in that skill (its `SKILL.md` Rules, its
`references/LESSONS.md`, or its own `references/FEEDBACK.md`); a note that holds for all goes here. Either way it is
written down in the same PR that acts on it — a rule with its date and the words it came from — and a rule a script can
check goes into that skill's check as well. A tool written for the job (a render loop, a probe, a check) goes into the
skill's `scripts/` or `tools/`, never only a scratch folder. (2026-11-28: "내가 하는 피드백은 전부 … 휘발되지않고
다음세션에도 적용되게 해줘 / 여기 로컬에만 있는 도구같은거있으면 다 올려주고".)

The fullest set is machine-wilds' (`https://github.com/jungrok5/machine-wilds/blob/main/references/FEEDBACK.md`); what
follows is the part of it that is not about that one game.

## Working

- Replies to the owner in Korean. What a piece shows on screen (titles, HUD, cards, names) is English unless the brief
  says otherwise; a Korean subtitle is not "corrected" into Korean or back without being asked.
- Merge only on the owner's word for that PR; when it comes, merge everything it covers. Always finish with a PR.
- Do not wait idle on a long render: run it in the background and do the rest meanwhile.
- Make, judge, improve on your own: key frames beside the previous version, fix what is weak, then show it. When
  effects or motion change, give the owner a short MP4 to look at.
- Spend what a job needs to get it right; a specialist agent for a specialist pass, judged by you.
- X posts are read through the mirror (CONTRIBUTING.md "X · Threads 게시물에서 출처 읽기"); never give up on an X link.
- ElevenLabs only after the owner says yes for that request (CLAUDE.md).

## Effects and motion

- Effects at AAA console-game quality — the way an Unreal or console game draws them, not photographs of the real
  thing (real electricity reads as hair).
- Variety is designed, not noise: no repeated pattern across instances or across characters (each arc, each machine,
  each burst its own shape, moment and rhythm).
- Particles only where the moment would make them (dust from loose ground under a heavy foot — not every step, not on
  grass), and nothing pops or steps between frames: an effect lives by the clock time of what made it, not by the
  current state of what made it; a slot held by a living particle is not taken back.
- Motion is judged per frame at the film's rate: an angle or a speed that jumps between two frames is a snap
  (`references/MOTION-QUALITY.md` 7).
- Big bodies in a film move; standing ones read as dead. They can notice the viewer's character (a slow glance).
- What carries a character fits what it passes through (a lift through a real hatch, flush at the top); a cut has a
  reason (no shot ends on a character walking into a wall).

## Films for social media

- A silent MP4, high quality, 2:00–2:20 for X (its limit is 2:20 and 512 MB; the same file goes to Threads and
  Facebook), watchable on a phone.
- The title card only at the end; the repository's address on every frame; © and no-reuse on the card
  (`MEDIA-LICENSE.md`).
- Show the range of what was made (morning to night, weathers, the far and the near), cut like a game's trailer.
- Post copy: one text for every platform, current (research what is posted on X now, here and abroad), "made with AI"
  as the hook, no technical breakdown.

## Words

- No AI tone in README, pages and posts: short and plain, technical terms welcome, nothing obvious explained; run the
  owner's de-AI-tone pass over new copy.
- No word that misleads: "assets", not "scans", for what is downloaded; no licence name that reads as the page's own
  when it is a source's; "made with AI", not "made by hand".
