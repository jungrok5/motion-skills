# LOOK — what makes it this style

**An ink shadow in a painted room.** The critter is the only flat thing in the frame: pure ink (#17131a, luminance ≤ 0.1)
with two white eyes and one accent colour. Everything around it is transparent watercolour over paper. The contrast is
the look; do not paint the critter, do not flatten the room.

## The critter
- Silhouette only: no inner lines, no shading on the black. Overlaps of black on black disappear on purpose.
- A big head (about half its height, 1.1 × 0.94 a), a small soft torso, thin capsule limbs, small oval feet, round hands.
- Eyes: two white ovals on the face side of the head (they slide and narrow as the head turns; gone from behind), dark
  pupils that lead the gaze, lids that close for blinks and come down for a glum look.
- Ears / tail / whiskers make the species (cat, bear, rabbit, mouse, fox, sprout, antenna; cat, fox, puff tail).
- One accent colour, painted (a gouache tile, deeper on the side away from the light): skirt, shorts, scarf, bag or
  hat. Choose it against the room's palette — it is the only saturated colour on the critter.
- In the room it is backlit: a thin warm rim on the edge facing the window (`rim`), a soft long shadow falling towards
  the camera (`shadow`), small contact shadows under the feet.

## The room
- One interior seen in one-point perspective, the camera at the critter's eye level or a little above (eye 500–520 at
  refZ 1000 for `a` ≈ 80–90), the horizon a little above the middle of a 3:4 frame.
- The window is at the back and is the light: bright gold-white, bars eaten by bloom, a glow that spills over the wall,
  a patch of the panes on the floor reaching towards the camera, dust turning in it.
- Washes (`wash`): three passes of a rough-edged shape, soft blotches of the same pigment (blooms), faint brush streaks,
  a darker wet edge, paper grain where the paint skips. Pen lines (`pen`): thin sepia, broken, a little loose — boards,
  frames, edges, wood grain.
- Palette per room: 2–3 related wall / floor browns or greens, the backlight colour, 1–2 accents in the clutter; deepen
  away from the window with `grade({ light, fallK })`.
- Clutter says who lives here (3–6 things) and one object in the foreground gives depth. Keep the floor around the
  critter's path clear and lit enough that the black reads.

## The frame
- 1080×1440, 24 fps, 7–16 s, one locked shot, fade in from and out to dark umber.
- The thing being tried is big enough to read on a contact sheet, with its moving part visible beside — not behind —
  the critter's head.
- No text in the picture. The caption is the post line beside it (the live page shows it under the canvas; the render
  writes `out/<name>.txt`).

## Sound
- Room tone always; birds / cicadas / rain / wind / a clock / a hum as the room suggests.
- Small sounds land on the frames that make them (press, beep, creak, slide, clunk, ding, thud); footsteps from heel
  strikes; a sigh or a small "hm" at most. The wait is the room tone alone.
