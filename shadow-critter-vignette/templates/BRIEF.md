## Topic
- The wrong idea: "I thought pressing it made toast." (the caption)
- Where it comes from: every morning a grown-up presses that lever and, a little later, toast jumps out.
- The thing: a toaster on a low bench. Without bread it does exactly what it always does — the lever goes down,
  ticks, and springs back up — and nothing comes out.
- Must not become: burnt toast or smoke, a lesson about bread, words on screen, the source film.
