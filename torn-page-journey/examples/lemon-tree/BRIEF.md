# BRIEF — The Long Way Home

Topic (proof of the skill, not from the source): "a fox carrying a lemon tree home, the long way round — a day of feelings".

## The ride
- Traveller: **Hana**, a small red fox — orange coat, cream chest and muzzle, black ear insides, a long brush tail with a
  white tip, a mustard beanie with a pom, a teal scarf. A blue step-through bicycle with a wicker basket.
  Carrying: a **potted lemon tree** (clay pot, a round crown, two lemons) — it sways with the ride and lags the bumps.
- Mass: light. She starts and settles quickly; the tail and scarf are what lag (spring on the speed, ~1.1 Hz, ζ .45).
- Idle: the chest breathes on a ~3 s irregular cycle, blinks every 2.7–4.9 s, ears twitch now and then.
- Arc: joy → curiosity → bustle → triumph → sorrow → relief → awe → wonder → (the morning again).

## The tune
8 bars (score.js `THEME`): 5-3-5-6…, chord roots I vi IV V I IV V I. Glockenspiel in joy, pizzicato in curiosity, accordion in
bustle, brass in triumph, felt piano in sorrow (minor), flute in relief (lydian), strings in awe (lydian), music box in wonder.

## Notes
