# LOOK — what makes a piece read as this style

Observed from the source: Chetaslua's post on X (https://x.com/chetaslua/status/2121885449509851415?a=8qELAlUgdwW0YiWOrYE&s=19,
read 2026-11-16): a 110 s, 1920×1080 film, "pure code, JS only", with "music + every sound" also code, of "a pelican riding a
bike from one art style <emotion> to the other". Stills were pulled every 3 s and read for their grammar. The pelican, its
scenes and its order are the reference's own and are never recreated; what is kept is the form below.

## The form

- **One traveller, one ride, no cuts.** A side-on travelling shot that never cuts away: the traveller stays in the left
  third, riding right, while the world scrolls past in parallax. Everything changes around them; they keep pedalling.
- **A chapter = one feeling painted in one medium.** Each stretch (8–12 s in the source) is a single emotion, and the art
  medium carries it: a child's crayon drawing for joy, a pencil sketchbook for a quiet, curious walk, flat gouache for
  a bustling market, a carved woodcut in three inks under a rayed sun for triumph, grey charcoal in rain for sorrow,
  wet watercolour with a rainbow for relief, a patterned folk painting for celebration, impasto oil at sunset for awe,
  a night harbour, a chalkboard with constellations for wonder.
- **The traveller is redrawn by every medium.** It keeps its silhouette, its colours and its one signature accessory
  (the source's blue scarf and red bike), but on the woodcut page it is carved, on the charcoal page it is grey with a
  faint scarf, on the chalk page it is chalk. It is always the same character; never a different design per page.
- **The page tears.** The next chapter arrives as a new sheet laid over the old one from the right, with a ragged torn edge,
  a white fibrous rim and a soft shadow on the page underneath. The edge sweeps over the traveller, so for a moment the
  front half is in the new medium and the back half in the old. No crossfades, no cuts.
- **The world matches the feeling, not just the colour.** Weather, light, time of day, the place and the creatures all
  move with the emotion: the storm comes with the sorrow, the rainbow with the relief, the moon and an owl with the wonder.
- **Small incidents, one per chapter.** A wave, a snap at a butterfly, the wings thrown open with no hands, a look up at
  the sky, a shiver in the rain. They are acted with a gather before and a settle after.
- **A companion per page.** A dog by its kennel, a rabbit at the fence, a cat on the quay, an owl in a bare tree — sitting,
  breathing, watching the traveller go by.
- **It loops.** The last page tears back into the first; the film can play forever.
- **Sound is one piece of music that changes its feeling** with each page (the same ride, a different band), the paper
  tears at every turn, and each place has its own air.

## What breaks it

- Cutting to a new scene instead of tearing a page over the old one; a crossfade instead of a tear.
- Two neighbouring chapters in the same medium, or a chapter whose medium does not fit its feeling.
- A traveller that becomes a different character on some page (new colours, no signature accessory), or one that is just
  pasted on top in a neutral style — it must be drawn BY the page's medium.
- A dead world: a backdrop that only scrolls, with nothing alive in it; a companion standing frozen.
- Wheels that skate (spin at the wrong rate for the ground passing), feet that slide off the pedals, legs that bend backwards.
- A score that is one loop under every page.
