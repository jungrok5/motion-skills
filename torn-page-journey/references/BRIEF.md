# BRIEF — from a topic to a journey

Write the brief before any code, as `BRIEF.md` in the project (`templates/BRIEF.md` is the blank). The topic can be a
story ("a fox carries a lemon tree home"), a day, a life, a trip, a product's journey, a song's verses, a list of feelings.

## 1 · Turn the topic into a ride
- **Who travels, on what, carrying what.** One traveller (a person, an animal, a creature) on a bicycle, and the one thing
  the story is about in the basket (a seedling, a letter, a cake, a lantern). Design the traveller new for the topic.
- **Mass and idle.** How heavy (sets timing: a light rider's scarf and tail lag a little and settle quickly; a big one
  starts and stops late), and what breathes at rest (chest, ears, tail, blinks).
- **The arc of feelings: 4–9 chapters.** One feeling each, in an order that is a story: a start, a struggle, a turn, an
  arrival. End on a feeling that leads back into the first (the film loops).

## 2 · For each chapter
| field | what to decide |
|---|---|
| feeling | one word (`joy`, `curiosity`, `bustle`, `triumph`, `sorrow`, `fear`, `relief`, `awe`, `wonder`, `calm`, `lonely`, `pride` — or your own with its own mood entry) |
| medium | the medium that carries THIS feeling (references/MEDIA.md) — or a new one for the topic; never the same as its neighbours |
| place | where the road goes: 2–4 depths (sky, far, middle, road, foreground), what grows there, the weather and the hour |
| the road | flat, uphill (effort: slower, standing on the pedals), downhill (coasting, feet up) |
| alive | one companion that sits and breathes (an animal, a person), and something that moves (birds, rain, fireflies, a kite) |
| incident | one gesture with its gather and settle: what she sees, what she does (`wave`, `look`, `catch`, `bell`, `flap`, `shiver`, `feetup`, or a new one) |
| stance and gaze | the mood's posture: leaning in, sitting up, hunched, head up at the sky |
| sound | the band for this feeling (from the mood), the place's bed (birds, crowd, rain, waves, crickets), one effect (thunder, an owl, gulls) |
| length | 6–10 s (shorter pages feel like a slideshow; longer ones lose the rhythm) |

## 3 · The tune
One tune for the whole film, 4–8 bars, written as scale degrees so each mood's mode re-colours it (major → minor → lydian).
It should be singable after one hearing.

## Reviews

**Cast sheet review** (`cast.html`, before any chapter): the traveller reads at 30 % size in every medium; colours and the
signature accessory survive every medium; facing right and left the chest, face, snout and toes are on the facing side,
the tail and pack behind, the chainring in front only when facing left; knees bend forward, elbows down; feet on the
pedals through the whole stroke; the idle breathes; every gesture gathers before it acts and settles after.
"Did the neck turn or the body?" — a yes fails.

**Still review** (each chapter mid-way, and each tear mid-way):
- Is this page unmistakably ONE medium, all of it — scenery, creatures and the traveller?
- Does the page feel like its word (would someone name the feeling without being told)?
- Is it different enough from the page before (light / dark, flat / textured, warm / cool)?
- At the tear: torn edge, white rim, shadow; the traveller half in each medium; nothing important cut by the edge.
- Does anything float, skate, or stand dead still? Does the gesture gather and land?
- Is the traveller in the left third with room ahead, head and cargo never cut?
- Is the world alive (something moves besides the scroll)?
