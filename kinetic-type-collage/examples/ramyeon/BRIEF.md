## The beat
128 bpm, root E1. Kick `x..x..x...x..x..`, snare `....x.......x..x`, hats in sixteenths with swing 0.1, bass `0..0..0...3..5..`.
Accents (crash + sub + stab) on `hungry`, `noodle`, `crack`, `bowl`. `bubble` drops to kick and bass; `stir` is hats
under a riser; the two egg half-beats are silent, so 탁 lands on the first hit after a gap; `end` rings out.

## Review
- **Prop sheet** — first pass: the pot's liquid was broth before any soup went in (→ a water tint); the egg white's
  second lobe was a full ball (a kit bug: `K.at` reset its squash → fixed in `lib/kit.js`); the timer's hub covered the
  4:30 (→ number moved below the hub, hand shortened); a bright ring under the egg (→ shadow bias). Everything read
  from four sides after that.
- **Stills** — first pass: `clock` showed only "3:4" (→ h 1.5); `boil` and `leek` bitmap words were cropped to blocks
  (→ fitted inside the frame); 물 was hidden by the pot (→ moved to the upper right); `egg-near` was a white egg on
  paper, i.e. nothing (→ ink ground); the packet was edge-on at the read moment (→ keyed turn instead of spin);
  국물까지 in paper white was unreadable over the noodles (→ ink); every top surface was overexposed (→ light
  calibrated in the engine, see references/LESSONS.md); 4분 30초 ran off the right edge (→ camera target moved).
- **Motion** (10 fps tile of the preview) — shots froze after their first third (→ the default ease now keeps creeping).
- **Check** — `check OK`: 20 shots, mean 0.70 s, paper + ink + greys 81 % of the film, strong colour 17 %.
