# BRIEF — 「The Keeper's Whale」

**One line**: An old lighthouse keeper wakes on a blank page, catches a flying pencil, draws a little coral boat — and the
sea spreads out from its waterline until a humpback breaches out of the horizon, blows a spout of coral fish, and the sky
is crayoned into a sunset on a sketchbook lying on a desk.

**Sound**: a music-box sea-shanty turn while he draws; whoosh and pop for the pencil; the sea's air from the horizon on; a
low bell and slow breath for the whale; a sparkle when its eye opens; splash, whoosh, flutter and sparkle for the spout of
fish; a swell and a chime into the sunset; the room at the desk; pencil scratches from every stroke.
