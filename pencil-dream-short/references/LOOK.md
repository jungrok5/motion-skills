# LOOK — what makes it read as this style

Observed from the reference (OscarAI's 30 s clip, https://x.com/Artedeingenio/status/2119895981403894112?a=_9q1hjQOFazzyl1wwv0&s=19),
frame by frame at 1 fps. The clip was made with image/video generators; these are the traits this skill rebuilds in code.

## The medium
- **Paper**: warm cream/grey sketch paper with a fine tooth, slightly darker toward the corners. Never white, never flat.
- **Graphite**: dark grey (not black) lines of varying weight; most contours are drawn twice — a firm line and a lighter,
  looser one beside it; lines overshoot their corners; hair and grass are single loose flicks. Small shading is parallel
  hatching (roofs) or scribble (dark windows).
- **One spot colour**: orange in the reference — the girl's glasses, a touch on her nose and lips, the flowers, the
  dragon's eyes, the butterflies. Laid on like a felt marker: streaky, a darker pooled edge, a little off the line.
  Everything else stays graphite until the very end.
- **Crayon at the end**: the last 5 s colour the sky with a yellow-orange coloured-pencil sunrise that fades out from
  the sun, grainy where the paper's tooth shows through. The colour family is the spot colour + its neighbour (yellow).

## The grammar (one continuous take)
1. **Wake** — a close-up of the hero alone on blank paper: blinks, glances left and right, a doodled "!" and "?" appear.
2. **The pencil** — a pencil arrives (drawing a curl of line as it flies), the hero's eyes follow it, the hero takes it.
3. **The first mark** — the hero draws something small in the spot colour within arm's reach (a flower), and smiles.
4. **The spread** — more of it appears along a ground line, then a house, a tree, birds, fields — farther things draw
   themselves; the camera pulls back and the hero shrinks into the landscape.
5. **The giant** — one huge sweeping arc rises behind the hero, then horns, a head, wings: a big friendly creature
   behind the hills, much larger than everything else.
6. **Its eyes** — the eyes are coloured in with the spot colour; it blinks; it smiles; the hero looks up.
7. **The swarm** — the spot colour lifts off the page: dozens of small spot-colour shapes swirl around the giant, in
   front of it and behind it; the giant closes its eyes, content.
8. **The wash** — crayon colour blooms over the sky from a sun behind the giant; the fields turn gold.
9. **The desk** — the camera pulls back: the whole drawing is a spread in a sketchbook on a dark wooden desk, with real
   coloured pencils lying around it. Held, then out.

## Proportions and acting
- The hero is a doodle: big round head (≈ 1/4 of the height), dot or simple round eyes, tiny nose and mouth, a stick-ish
  body with a loose shirt, thin limbs. Readable as a silhouette even when 60 px tall.
- Expressions are small and clear: curious (one brow), surprised (o mouth), delighted (open smile), content (closed eyes).
- The giant is friendly: big eyes, a smile, slow movements (breathing, blinking), never threatening.

## Camera
- Starts as a bust close-up, ends at about 2× the spread with the desk around it. In between it only pulls back and
  drifts up toward the giant. A slight roll appears only once the desk (the "real" world) is in view.

## What NOT to copy
The reference's girl (round orange glasses, bob), its dragon, its flowers and butterflies, its village. Each topic gets its
own hero, spot item, first mark, world, giant and swarm.
