# BRIEF — 「The Kite」 (30 s)

**One line** — A pencil boy on a sketchbook at a café table on a sunny square is drawn a slide by a real pencil, rides it
off the page and down to the cobbles, and catches a drawn kite that turns into a real red tissue kite and takes off on the wind.

## The place
- HDRI `palermo_square` (Poly Haven, CC0): a sunny square with pale stone façades and a blue sky — room for a kite. Turned 200°.
- Ground `patterned_cobblestone` (grey fan-pattern setts, matching the square's own); table: round café table, `wood_table_worn`, pedestal.
- Sketchbook (A4, spiral) near the table's right edge, turned 6°; the way out is to the right.

## The hero — Pip
- A small boy in a cap: short hair, round eyes, button nose; a sweater with two stripes, shorts, boots. 7 cm tall.
- Graphite only; the cap is laid in with graphite tone.
- **Mass / idle**: light and quick, but steps, gathers and lands with weight; breathes, shifts his weight on a slower
  rhythm, blinks irregularly, looks around at the square.

## What turns real
A diamond kite drawn at the size of the real one (KITE in props.js); the real kite is built in code (tissue membrane bowed
between two bamboo spars, a tail of three paper bows, a sagging string), revealed from his hands outward.

## Motion reference
Hand-keyed (lib/gait.js footholds, hero.js poses); no generated video.

## Sound
Music box for Pip (a small hum, a climbing run, a falling run down the slide); the square's air rising with the wind;
pencil scratches from the strokes; steps; a whoosh down the slide and a thump on landing; a sparkle and a felt-piano chord
at the reveal; flutter as the kite rises; paper at the close.
