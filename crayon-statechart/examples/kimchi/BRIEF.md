# BRIEF — the making of kimchi (김장)

**Topic (as the user said it):** "how kimchi is made, in this style" (the worked example; not the reference's topic)
**What the viewer should understand at the end:** napa cabbage is salted, seasoned with a red paste, packed into jars,
and keeps changing in the jar while it is eaten all winter — until next year's kimjang.
**Facts checked against:** common practice of winter kimjang: halving cabbages, salting by hand or soaking in brine,
resting until the leaves bend, rinsing three times; a paste of radish, chili flakes, garlic, fish sauce, sugar; neighbours
working together on the floor; onggi jars; kimchi ripening in the cold and souring in spring; sour kimchi into stew.
