# BRIEF — template and rubric

Write the project's `BRIEF.md` with these headings before any code. `check.mjs` reads **Never drawn**.
The worked example (`examples/held-breath/BRIEF.md`) shows every section filled.

```md
# BRIEF — <title> · <English title>

## The story (as given)
> the user's words, unedited (or the list of feelings they gave)

## Feeling inventory
| beat | feeling | weight | temperature | motion | light |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| … one row per beat of the story; weight 1–5 … |
Top feelings (what a stranger should name): **…**, **…**, **…**

## Never drawn
- every person, object, place, brand, text, symbol in the story — one line per group, comma-separated
  (these words must not name anything in art.js)

## Translation
| feeling | carried by |
|---|---|
| … | weather / light / season / time of day / place / colour / a crowd of small things / a motion / a mark |

## The one picture
One line: place, time, light, weather. Eye path: (1) → (2) → (3).

## Composition (16:9, every pixel worked)
Horizon height · big masses · focal point (thirds) · darkest dark beside lightest light · what fills each corner.

## Palette
Ramps as hex, named by role (light, sky, shadow, accents). Paper colour and why. Colour logic in one line.

## Tools (3–5)
| tool | role | why |

## Order of work (stages)
block-in → … → accents

## References
Folders given; what was taken (palette, mark types, pressure); what was not (subjects, compositions).

## Review log
| round | still | score /20 | what changed |
```

## Rubric (score each 0–2; ship at 16+ with no 0)

| # | item | 2 = | 0 = |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **feeling first** | someone who has not read the story names at least two of the top feelings | they name a thing, or nothing |
| 2 | **nothing literal** | nothing in the picture can be pointed at as a beat of the story | a person, object, word or symbol from the story is there |
| 3 | **human hand** | at 100 % every area is marks: direction, pressure, colour vary; edges are strokes | even fields, smooth gradients, vector edges |
| 4 | **every pixel** | corners and edges are worked as carefully as the centre | bare paper, a dead band, an empty corner |
| 5 | **values** | at 160 px wide the picture reads: big masses, one focal point | grey, or everything the same weight |
| 6 | **colour** | one harmony; shadows coloured; warm light; no mud | grey shadows, muddy mixes, neon |
| 7 | **depth** | far is cooler, paler, smaller-marked; near is warmer, darker, bigger-marked | everything on one plane |
| 8 | **detail at the focus** | the smallest, sharpest marks are where the eye lands | detail spread evenly like a texture |
| 9 | **its own picture** | not the starter, the example, the source's sea town, or a reference picture | a re-skin of something that exists |
| 10 | **would you hang it** | yes | no |

Questions to ask of every still: *What do I feel first? What do I see first? Is that the same thing the brief wanted?
Is there anything I could name from the story? Where is the paper bare? Where does it look like a computer drew it?*
