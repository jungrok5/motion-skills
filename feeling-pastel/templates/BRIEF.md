## The one picture

Rice paddies at the end of a summer night, a tall cloud glowing faintly, fireflies over the water.
Eye: the cloud's lit edge (1) → its reflection in the paddies (2) → the fireflies (3).

## Composition

Horizon at 62 %. Cloud upper left third. Paddies in bands that narrow toward the horizon; dikes as dark lines.
Grass in the lower corners. Every corner worked.
