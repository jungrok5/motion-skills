# BRIEF — from a topic to a poster film

Write the project's `BRIEF.md` from this template **before any code**. A topic is a person, an event, a brand, a
launch, a club, a book, a word. The brief turns it into a poster that only that topic could have, then into the film.

## Template

```
# <title>
Topic: <one sentence: who / what / when>          Posted on: <Reels / TikTok / X / a site>   Language: <ko / en / …>

## The poster
Hero line:   <3–7 Latin characters, or 2–4 Hangul syllables — the word the film is about>   on the accent band
Subline:     <5–16 characters, completes the thought>
Small lines: <top left (name / brand) · top right (date / role / place)>, repeated above the footer?
Giant crop:  <2–4 characters cropped by the sheet — texture (initials, a number, a hanja, a symbol)>
Running text:<250–700 characters in the topic's voice — it repeats to fill columns>
Footer:      <what sits at the bottom — the shredder bites the bottom half on camera>
Picture:     <what, drawn how (in code: shapes / gradients; or a photo); object (cut-out, best) or photo mode>
Topic marks: <anything only this topic has: a grid, proof marks, a stamp, a ticket stub, a route, a score…>
Grid sketch: <rows top→bottom with their y in poster units (1000 × 1500)>
Fonts:       display <family weight> · text <family weight> — and why for this topic
Palette:     paper <#> · ink <#> · accent <#> (one) — contrast ink:paper ≥ 4.5, ink:accent ≥ 2

## The film
Cut:         <strips 20–32> · jitter <0.15–0.25>      Landing order: <top / bottom / middle> — why
Floor:       <concrete / a desk / board> tint <#>      Machine body: <#>     Inset ground: <# (often the paper)>
Beats:       hook 0–0.5 · cascade –2.37 · machine –4.4 · rebuild –8.0  (change only with a reason)
Grade:       warm <0–1.5>      Sound: bright <0.8–1.2> · body <0.8–1.2>   (thin stock crisper, card heavier)
```

## Review A — the poster (score `out/poster.png`, each 0–2; ship at ≥ 12 of 14, no 0)
1. **Only this topic.** Could the poster belong to another topic with the words swapped? (2 = no.)
2. **Reads at a glance.** The hero line reads in a thumbnail; the hierarchy is clear in one look.
3. **Printed matter.** A strict grid, real type, rules, a halftone, running text — a print, not a slide.
4. **One accent**, used for the hero band (and at most one more topic mark). No second accent colour.
5. **Big type is big.** The hero and the footer span the sheet; the giant crop is cropped hard.
6. **The picture survives shredding**: high-contrast round shapes, a clear silhouette, not a busy scene.
7. **No issues**: nothing off the sheet or over its box, no descender hanging into the next line, contrast met.

## Review B — the stills (`--sheet`, `--stills 0,1.3,3.9,6.2`; each 0–2; ship at ≥ 10 of 12)
1. **Frame 0** is the finished poster, sharp and readable, sitting above the centre of the frame.
2. **Hook**: strips rip in a wave from the top; the lunge ends on the hero line.
3. **Cascade**: the hero ribbon carries the whole hero word in the accent, in focus; ribbons overlap without passing
   through each other; arches glow where light comes through.
4. **Machine**: the poster reads above the discs at the start; the bite is visible; under the cutters the sliced footer
   is legible in the curtain; the inset sits on its ground.
5. **Rebuild**: strips arrive from both sides, the wave accelerates, the last frame equals frame 0.
6. **Material**: paper looks like paper (fibre, satin ink, soft shadow), not plastic or a screen.
