# LOOK — what makes the style

**The poster is the protagonist.** The film never shows anything that is not the poster or the machine that eats it.
It works because the poster is good print design first: Swiss-school discipline (a strict grid, flush-left type, rules,
small lines in the corners), one giant word, type so big the sheet crops it, a halftone picture and running text that
only needs to read well when someone pauses. One accent colour behind the hero line — that restraint is the look.

**Four shots, one clock (60 fps, 8 s).**
| shot | time | what happens | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| hook | 0.00–0.50 | 4 held frames of the finished poster (readable in frame 0), then the strips rip sideways in a fast wave from the top while the camera lunges (an ease-in power 2.2) at the hero line | the first frame of a reel must read; the rip is the promise |
| cascade | 0.50–2.37 | ribbons in three depth layers fall and lay down on a pile; the hero ribbon — the hero rows cut lengthwise — lands at 0.3 s and runs along the camera path; the camera tracks with a speed ramp, rolled −40°, shallow focus | texture and speed; the hero word survives as one long ribbon |
| machine | 2.37–4.40 | one move: a fast dolly at the poster going in bottom-first, a beat on the bite, a dive past the cutters with a slight orbit and roll, under the comb looking up into the curtain as the sliced footer comes out, a truck; an inset (78 %) on a plain ground that grows in over 8 frames | the cause; the inset changes the rhythm |
| rebuild | 4.40–8.00 | 26 strips arrive scrambled from both sides, coast, land in an accelerating wave with a small overshoot (a spring), neighbours shiver, three strips lift as they land; a soft light passes down after the last click; a 0.34 s hold with a slow push that continues into the hook | the payoff, and the seam |

**The edit.** Directional whips on the four frames before and two after each cut (4, 10, 20, 34 px out; 24, 10 px in);
a punch on the rip (a 1.2 % zoom bump with a few px of shake and a sharpen pulse) and on the final click (0.7 %).

**Paper.** Matte stock (roughness ≈ 0.6) with satin ink (≈ 0.44), a fibre normal map, light coming through thin stock
from behind (translucency ≈ 0.25), the print showing faintly on the back (≈ 16 %), crease folds (sharp |d| ridges, so the
light breaks on lines), a slow bow, lifted edges, ragged cut edges, the two top strips crumpled more. Shredder ribbons
carry transverse crimp from the rollers. Ribbons never pass through each other: each is laid on a height map of the
ones before it, the lift draping with a limited slope.

**Light.** Poster shots: one soft key from the upper left with a narrow spread (a pool, darker corners) that drifts up
the poster while it assembles, a cool sky fill, concrete or a desk below. Cascade: a smaller key (readable shadows of
falling ribbons), a broad sky, a back light glowing through arches. Machine: a front-left key, a rim from behind
through the strips, steel in a soft studio.

**Finish.** Sub-frame motion blur (180° shutter) for everything, Khronos PBR Neutral, a slightly deeper black point, a
gentle S, cool shadows and warm highlights, a highlight glow, a soft vignette, fine grain (never on the inset ground).

**Sound.** Built from the render's own timings: a rip per strip, a whoosh with the lunge, a swish and a pat per landing
ribbon, the motor sagging under load with the cutting grain, a hiss for each strip coasting home, a click on every
landing (the last one heavier with a low thump), room tone; −14 LUFS integrated, −1 dBFS ceiling, the tail folded onto
the start so it loops.
