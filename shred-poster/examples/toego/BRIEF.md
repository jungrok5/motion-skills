# 퇴고 — 토요 퇴고 모임
Topic: a Saturday-afternoon writing circle that brings drafts, reads them aloud and rewrites them line by line.
Posted on: Instagram Reels / X.   Language: ko.

Why this topic suits the style: revising *is* cutting a text into lines and putting them back in a better order — the
film's rip, shred and rebuild are the circle's work. The rebuild lands **from the middle outwards**: one sentence gets
fixed first, then the lines around it.
