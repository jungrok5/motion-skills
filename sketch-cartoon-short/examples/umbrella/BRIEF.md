# BRIEF — 「The Runaway Umbrella」

TITLE        The Runaway Umbrella
LOGLINE      On a rainy day the wind steals a boy's red umbrella; he and his dog chase it across the town until he leaps from a
             bridge, catches it in a storm cloud and floats home under it.
HERO         Juno, a lanky boy (~12), a mop of dark hair, an open ochre raincoat over a mustard sweater, rolled grey-teal
             trousers, rubber boots. Reads by the coat flaring behind him and the hair.
COMPANION    Biscuit, a small scruffy cream dog, pointed ears, a fluffy tail; alarmed first, follows everywhere, waits on the bridge
FORCE        the wind (drawn as curling gust lines) and a scribble storm cloud that swallows the umbrella — no face, it is weather
PLACES       the blank page · a street corner with a lamp (K.facade, K.lamp) · a long street of facades for the chase
             (K.street, K.skyline far) · two roofs with a gap (drawn in the page with S.shape + K.roofs) · an arched bridge
             under a skyline (K.bridge) · the town from above (K.skyline, K.roofs)
ACCENT       dusty red (#c48a7c): the umbrella; echoed only in the shop awnings
PROPS        the umbrella (umbrella.js): hooked handle, shaft, a six-rib canopy that folds, opens and blows inside out
STUNT        two: the rooftop leap (run → crouch at the edge → arc → squash landing, the dog leaping after) and the catch
             (run along the parapet → crouch → leap up → the hand meets the falling handle → it snaps open)
SCENE        1. the blank page draws Juno, Biscuit and the umbrella; it starts to rain
             2. a gust turns the umbrella inside out and tears it from his hand; shock; he sets his jaw
             3. the chase down the street, the umbrella tumbling ahead
             4. across the roofs: the leap over the gap, the dog right behind
             5. on the bridge a scribble cloud grows and swallows the umbrella; thunder
             6. he runs, leaps off the parapet, catches the umbrella as the cloud spits it out — it opens, the cloud bursts into sheets
             7. he floats down over the town under the open umbrella, sheets drifting around him
             8. back on the blank page he lands, Biscuit runs in and sits; one last drop; a wink; the pencil draws the ground
SOUND        pencil scratches for the drawing; rain; a tiptoe pizzicato after the theft; a quick oom-pah chase with a reed tune
             and brushes; the music drops for the cloud (scribble growl, thunder); a fwump + chime + major swell at the catch;
             a slow felt-piano waltz for the float; a small button at the end
QUALITY      rough doubled lines that boil; fills off the line; paper visible; the umbrella the only warm thing; feet on
             the ground while running; the umbrella's canopy in frame whenever he holds it
MUST NOT     the reference clip's girl, pet or scribble creature with eyes; saturated colour; vector-clean lines; a hero floating
             above the roofs while "running"
