# BRIEF — Oma's apple cake

- **What the page is**: a grandmother's apple-cake recipe card, knitted by her as a kitchen wall hanging — the recipe she
  never wrote down, except in wool.
- **What it does**: a servings stepper (− / +, `data-hic-click` on +) rewrites the quantities ("4 apples · 200 g butter",
  25 g butter and half an apple per serving); "Oven 45:00" starts a real countdown whose digits re-knit every second;
  "Pull a thread" (`data-knit-unravel`) unravels it; underneath, "Knit it back up" (`data-knit-back`).
- **Words**: "Oma's / apple cake" 120 px headline (editable); "SERVES" 62 px caps; quantities 62 px caps, two items only
  (the eggs were cut: the line did not read); button labels 54–60 px caps with .07 em spacing.
- **Yarns**: oatmeal `--bg #eadcc0`, cinnamon `--ink #5a2a1c`, apple red `--accent #b8312a`, leaf `--leaf #5f7d3a`; one
  colourway; `palette` = those four (selection knits in leaf green).
- **Yarn and gauge**: cotton — `thick .23, ply 4, twist .7, fuzz .35, sheen .32, sparkle .1, wobble .12`; stitch 10 × 8,
  card 1200 × 900 = 120 × 112 stitches. (First tried a chunky 12 × 10: the small lines turned to mush; see LESSONS.)
- **Motifs**: a band of apples with stems and leaves between ink rules, top and bottom; one big intarsia apple (24 columns,
  generated round with two lobes, stem, leaf and a highlight in `--bg`).
- **Table and lamp**: blue-and-white gingham kitchen cloth (`#eef0f1` / `#6f93c4`, 8-stitch checks — the first, stronger
  blue was too loud next to the red); a warm window lamp high left (`floor .55`).
- **Needles**: steel (`#b9bec6`, metal 1) with apple-red knobs.
- **The reveal**: Oma's own card on lined paper in a hand-like cursive: "Oma's secret — brown the butter first, until it
  smells of hazelnuts. One spoon of sour cream in the batter. Don't tell Uncle Willi." Placed left; the heap lands bottom
  right (`heap .84, .78`), clear of the card and its button.
- **Phone layout**: 720 × 1300 (72 × 162 stitches): title, apple below it, servings, quantities in two lines, both buttons
  full width.
- **Hint and sound**: "Type in the title · pull the loose thread"; default pitch.
- **Still review notes**: idle — every line reads after the gauge change; "PULL A THREAD" clipped its last letter at 56 px
  → 54 px with less padding. bare — note and heap apart, the heap shows cream, cinnamon, red and green yarn. Check: all pass.
