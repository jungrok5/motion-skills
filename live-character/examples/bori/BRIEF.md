# BRIEF — 보리 (a Jindo puppy)

**Topic (as given):** "강아지도 되게 해줘" — make the lively feel work for a dog.

**Who:** 보리, a five-month-old Korean Jindo puppy, the yellow coat (황구). Curious, eager, easily delighted; follows
whatever moves with its nose first. Not a person in a dog suit: it never waves, it gives a paw.

**Framing:** full body, sitting, facing the viewer; the ground line at y 950. 4:5 stage.

**Silhouette:** a big round head (puppy proportions: head ≈ 40 % of the height), upright triangle ears, cheek fluff,
a pear-shaped sitting body, folded haunches at the sides, two straight front legs, a curled tail over the right side.

**Colour:** coat honey-orange `#e2a35a`, cream mask, bib and leg fronts `#fbe8c8`, dark nose, a red collar with a
brass name tag — the one warm accent besides the coat.

**Face:** round glossy dark eyes (no anime iris), cream "maro" brow dots that lift and tilt, a protruding muzzle (the
deepest point of the head: it leads every turn), ω mouth, a tongue that hangs out when panting.

**What moves (per the recipe):**
- head on the bulge, muzzle depth 0.38, nose 0.55 — a small turn reads strongly
- ears: stiff chains (`ear`), perk / flatten by `earL` / `earR`; flick in idle
- tail: a curled chain, wagging by `wag` (always a little; a lot when happy)
- front legs: planted limbs (IK keeps the paws on the ground while the body breathes and leans); "손!" lifts the right
  paw toward the viewer, the lower leg hanging back from the raised elbow (the quadruped fold)
- collar tag: a short pendulum
- breath faster than a person's (temper.breath 1.3), springs bouncier (1.2), idle moves every 6–12 s

**Idle moves:** look around, glance, look down, ear flick, sniff, head tilt, a panting spell, a yawn.
**Actions:** 갸웃 (dog tilt), 꼬리 (wag), 손! (give paw), 멍! (bark), 쓰담 (eyes shut, ears back, wag), 깜짝, 도리도리, 깡총.
**Expressions:** 기본, 신남 (^^ eyes, wag, pant), 놀람 (ears up), 시무룩 (ears down, no wag), 졸림.

**Demo (18 s):** follows a pointer with eyes then head → tilts at it → barks "멍! 멍멍!" → gives a paw → gets happy →
barks → is patted → gets sleepy.

**Still review — fail any frame where:** a paw floats or slides; the lifted paw folds forward; an ear tears from the
head; the tail detaches from the body; the muzzle and eyes disagree on where the head points.
