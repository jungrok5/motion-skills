# BRIEF — 하루 (a girl in a raincoat)

**Topic:** the first character of the skill: "make a character move naturally, like the LivLiv clip" — an original
person, not any LivLiv character.

**Who:** 하루, a calm, quick-to-react girl on a rainy day. Notices you first with her eyes.
**Framing:** waist-up, 4:5; the cut below the coat pockets.
**Silhouette:** chestnut bob with long side locks and an ahoge; a boxy mustard raincoat with a hood behind the neck.
**Colour:** chestnut hair, teal eyes, mustard coat; the accent is the leaf hair clip (green) and white drawstrings.
**Face:** `animeEye` (teal), thin brows that show through the bangs, a small mouth with Hangul lip sync, blush.
**What moves:** bangs (9 locks), two side locks each side, three back sections, the ahoge, two drawstrings; arms with
wave, cheer, think (fist to chin), clasp (shy), hips, tuck (hair behind the ear).
**Temperament:** default; idle moves every 10–18 s, including tuck and fidget.
**Actions:** 손 흔들기, 끄덕, 도리도리, 갸웃, 깡총, 만세, 생각, 깜짝, 수줍, 쓰담. **Expressions:** 기본, 웃음, 놀람, 시무룩, 부끄.
**Demo (19 s):** follows the pointer → waves and says hello → smiles and nods → thinks → cheers → gets shy.
**Still review:** the side locks stay attached on tilts; the fist reaches the chin, not the collar; a raised arm never
shows a joint ball at the shoulder; the coat hem never shows on a hop.
