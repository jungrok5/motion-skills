# BRIEF — 고요의 바다 기지 (Tranquility Base)

A worked example on a topic the source world does not have: the Moon instead of a green valley, a rover instead of a
train, a Korean crew and Korean log lines, a black sky with stars. Everything in the style list of `references/BRIEF.md`
stays; everything below was designed for this topic.

## Topic
A six-wheeled lunar rover drives from the landing field across the Sea of Tranquility to a base on a crater rim, while
a crew of twelve builds the base just ahead of it. At the end the camera rises over the base and looks back across the
plain to the Earth on the horizon.

## The line and the traveller
- **Traveller:** a white pressurised rover (six gold wheels, a cab with dark windows, a mast and a small dish) towing a
  cargo trailer. Dust, not smoke: low grey puffs behind the wheels, no chuff — an electric hum.
- **Line (≈ 300 m, 18 s build):** landing field → solar farm → mining ground among craters → up a ramp onto the crater
  rim → stops at the hub dome.
- **Track:** not rails — the paver lays hexagonal sintered-regolith tiles (a real lunar construction idea) just ahead of the
  wheels; the lamplighter plants orange route beacons on both sides.

## Look
- Sky: black, stars, a hard white sun low on the side (long shadows getting longer through the build).
- Ground: mid-grey mare regolith, lighter ejecta round crater rims, darker crater floors, pale highlands on the sides.
- Things: white and gold-foil hardware, dark-blue panels, orange beacons; the greenhouse glows green, lamps warm.
- The coral wireframe build stays the accent (it reads even better on grey).
- End: base lamps and beacons glow; the camera turns round to the Earth, a blue-and-white half disc on the horizon.

## Review (score each still 0–2; ship at ≥ 12/14, no 0)
1. Reads as the Moon at a glance (black sky, grey ground, craters) — not a grey valley.
2. Every cursor is on something being built, and its Korean line says what.
3. The tile road is always just ahead of the rover; nothing beside the line is still a wireframe when the rover passes.
4. Each stretch reads as its own place (pads → panels → rigs → domes).
5. Tags and log readable at 390 px (vertical cut) and at 1080p; no tag stuck over the HUD.
6. The final shot: the whole base, the line back across the plain, the Earth on the horizon.
7. Same style as valley-line: low-poly, flat shading, coral wireframe → dithered solid, the same HUD, marks and cursors.
