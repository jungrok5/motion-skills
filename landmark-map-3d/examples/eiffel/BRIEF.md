## The landmark
- **Real dimensions:** base 125 m square; 1st floor 57.6 m (deck ~70 m); 2nd floor 115.7 m (~40 m); top platform
  276 m (~19 m); antennas to 330 m. Leg sections shrink from ~25 m at the ground to merge at the 2nd floor.
- **Parts, bottom to top:** four stone piers → four lofted lattice legs leaning in on a curved profile → the four
  decorative arches under the 1st floor → 1st-floor deck with its lattice frieze, railing and glass pavilions → legs
  merging → 2nd-floor deck and glass room → one lattice shaft narrowing to the top → top platform, cabin, lantern,
  red-and-white antenna mast.
- **Signature details:** the concave leg curve, the arches, the X-braced lattice you can see through (alpha-cut, so it
  casts lattice shadows), the platforms' frieze.
- **Material:** bronze-brown iron (#a27e64), darker solid edges; **at night:** warm gold floodlight (emissive), gold
  bulbs along every edge, 900 white sparkle points flashing 15–18.5 s, two beams turning from the lantern.
