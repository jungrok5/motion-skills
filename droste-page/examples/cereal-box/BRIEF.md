# BRIEF — Spiral Crunch (the back of the box)

Topic: the back panel of a cereal box, printed with a picture of the back of this box — the original Droste joke (a
cocoa tin with a nurse holding the tin) on the shelf of a breakfast table. Not the source's subject (a tile wall).

## Window
- S: 2.5  centre: (0.5, 0.53) landscape, (0.5, 0.5) portrait  frame: `print` (red band, white keyline, blue rule), 1.6 U  radius: 0

## Content
- Editable line: the brand slab "SPIRAL CRUNCH" (red slab, white keyline, blue drop shadow) at the top; types itself on
  open. Rename the cereal and every box down the spiral is renamed.
- Clickables: six cereal loops on a "Bite the loops!" puzzle panel left of the window; a click takes a bite (a cream
  circle out of the ring) or puts it back, updates "n of 6 bitten"; notes 0–5 on the card preset.
- Counter: a red starburst "COLLECT BOX Nº n", top right; start 1 (the box filling the screen at rest).
- Wind control: "Twist-O-Meter — Twist the box", ticks flat / once / twice.
- Hint "SCROLL TO FALL IN"; sound toggle "CRUNCH OFF / CRUNCH ON" in the blue foot strip.
