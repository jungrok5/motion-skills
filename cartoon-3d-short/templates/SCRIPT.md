## Script (~3.7 words/s, 55–70 s, ~200–240 words; one male narrator, steady, no pauses)
1. **Hook** — the absurd fact stated flat, sentence one, 10–16 words.
2. **Setting** — year, place, a named everyman or the pride of the town; 1–2 lines, present tense.
3. **Escalation** — 3–6 beats, each a little bigger; others laugh / doubt; 1–2 exaggeration jokes.
4. **Mystery** — "But wait, …?"
5. **"You see,"** — the mechanism through the analogy.
6. **Consequence** — what changed because of it.
7. **Twist** — the last line lands the irony; the video ends on the last word.

## Draft
Your toast is not unlucky. It is doing physics. You knock it off the table, and it starts to spin.
You see, a normal table is only tall enough for half a turn. So the butter lands face down, almost every time.
Want it to land butter side up? Get a taller table.
