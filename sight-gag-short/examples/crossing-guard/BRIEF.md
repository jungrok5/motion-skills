Topic from the user: *"a school crossing guard"* — designed here as the worked example of the skill (a topic that is
not in the reference clip: the reference hero is a mad-scientist type with a tan leather glove fixing six street
nuisances; this one shares only the format and the look).

## 1. Hero
- **Who:** Walt, a lanky retired school crossing guard. Deadpan — never runs, never smiles until the last beat.
- **Silhouette:** tall and stooped, white walrus moustache, peaked guard cap, hi-vis vest over a navy sweater,
  orange rain boots, a lollipop STOP paddle always in his left hand.
- **Machine on his back:** a red tank with an amber beacon that blinks (`extras: ["contraption", "beacon"]`), steam
  puffing from the vent.

## 2. Gadget
- **What:** a giant white cartoon glove (three stitched lines on the back, grey cross-stitched cuff) on a
  red-and-white striped hose — a crossing pole that stretches.
- **Why it fits the topic:** a crossing guard's whole job is a raised hand.
- **Its personality:** polite, precise, a little smug; it does every job one notch more than needed.

## 5. Title (the pun)

## 6. Sound

hook) → groove → a beat of silence on the scooter hit and on the car hit → tiptoe while Granny shuffles → groove for
the balloon → outro with a brass button. Foley from the shots' sfx marks.

## 7. Must not look like (the reference, or a bad version of this)

- a lone raised finger facing the camera (it reads as the middle finger — scold by pointing, spin on a loose hand);
- characters below ~35 % of the frame height; a lamp post in front of a punchline; a glove that pops out of frame.

## Stills rubric (score 0–2 each at the marks; fix anything under 2)
1. **The gag reads with the sound off** in one still at its `act` mark.
2. **The glove is the actor:** shape and pose say the intention (stop, pinch, flick, grab, scold, thumbs up).
3. **The hero is deadpan** and stays in frame; the glove never fully leaves frame mid-gag.
4. **The hose reads as one continuous stretch** from the tank to the cuff, no kinks, sagging when slack.
5. **Grounded:** contact shadows, no feet under the pavement, held props stay in hands.
6. **The look:** bright sun from the left, blue-violet shadows, painted brick and chips, saturated shopfronts.
7. **Faces read** at film size: eyes, lids, brows, mouth.
8. **No accidental rude gestures**, no brand names, no real people.
